## How to structure it

### X
- **Format:** long post, text first. The hook has to work in the first 2 lines before "Show more".
- **Media:** attach a 30-60s clip of Hector from Lenny's podcast, or quote-post Victor's video instead of linking it. Native media beats links on X.
- **Links:** keep them out of the main post and put them in reply 1 and reply 2. X pushes posts with external links down.
- **Tags:** tag @hectorserrranoo and @victorgu0 in the body. If either reposts, that's your reach. Tag @lennysan only in the reply, so it doesn't look like you're chasing him.
- **Timing:** reply to the first 5-10 comments within the hour.

### LinkedIn
- **Format:** text plus native video. Upload the video, don't paste a YouTube link.
- **Video:** a 45-60s talking-head of you is best. It's original content and the algorithm favours it. Script:
  1. (0-5s) Hook: "Your job description would reject the two best growth marketers in SaaS. They're both 19."
  2. (5-25s) Hector: 400 creatives a week, 1 in 1,000 creators, Wispr Flow at $2B
  3. (25-40s) Victor: 280M views, MrBeast format, 14,000 videos
  4. (40-55s) The point: "They don't have years. They have reps."
  5. (55-60s) "Would your hiring process let them in?"
- **Clip option:** if you use a clip from Lenny's podcast instead, keep it under 60s and credit it on screen. It's someone else's content, so your own video is safer.
- **Links:** only in the first comment.
- **Tags:** tag Hector, Victor, Wispr Flow and Composio by typing @ in the composer. Keep it to 4-5 tags. More than that looks spammy.
- **Ending:** the closing question is there to get comments. Reply to every comment in the first 90 minutes.

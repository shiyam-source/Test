## Post

Here's the dirty secret of AEO: you can't tie it to revenue. Not cleanly. Not yet.

So it only works when two things are true at the same time:

1. Your execs *really* believe AEO matters. Enough to keep funding it without a clean ROI number.
2. Your AEO team is *really* good. Because nobody can independently check their work.

How many companies have both?

Not many.

And it's not a skill issue. It's a measurement issue. We ran 1,000 B2B software questions through ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude and Gemini. Here's what we found:

→ ChatGPT showed a link on just 8% of top-of-funnel answers. Your buyer learns about you, and there's no click to track.

→ 74% of the pages AI engines cited weren't in Google's top 10 or AI Overview. Your SEO tools can't see most of what's happening.

→ All three link-showing engines agreed on 0.09% of citations. "Are we winning in AI search?" has a different answer on every engine.

So most teams end up stuck in one of three boxes:

Starved. Great team, skeptical execs. Good work gets cut the minute someone asks, "Can you tie this to pipeline?"

Expensive guessing. Execs are all in, but the team can't prove anything. Lots of dashboards. No answers.

Waiting. No budget, no owner. Waiting for a competitor to prove it first.

The fix isn't more faith. It's proof both sides can read.

That's what we built Slate to do.

For the exec: a weekly report on where you show up in AI answers, which pages get cited, and the sessions those pages bring in.

For the team: Slate does the tracking and a lot of the work. Every change is logged and reversible, so the work is easy to check.

Will it give you perfect attribution? No. Nobody can yet. But it swaps "trust me" for a paper trail.

Be honest: which box is your company in?

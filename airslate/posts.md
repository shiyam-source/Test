airSlate makes $200M a year from SEO.

Its CEO just said the playbook has 9–12 months left.

pdfFiller, signNow, DocHub, US Legal Forms. All of them run on one search machine:

→ 35M pages across 4.8B tracked keywords
→ 37,000 "DocHub vs X" comparison pages
→ One plain PDF site doing $106M a year

The 3 levers I'd steal tomorrow:

𝟭. 𝗚𝗼𝗼𝗴𝗹𝗲'𝘀 𝘀𝘂𝗴𝗴𝗲𝘀𝘁𝗶𝗼𝗻𝘀 𝗮𝘀 𝘁𝗵𝗲 𝗰𝗼𝗻𝘁𝗲𝗻𝘁 𝗽𝗹𝗮𝗻
"rental agreement" → "rental agreement for a room in Texas" → a page with that exact title. ~120K pages built this way.

𝟮. 𝗔 "𝘃𝘀" 𝗽𝗮𝗴𝗲 𝗳𝗼𝗿 𝗲𝘃𝗲𝗿𝘆 𝗰𝗼𝗺𝗽𝗲𝘁𝗶𝘁𝗼𝗿
Buyers comparing tools are close to paying. Own that page and you're inside the decision.

𝟯. 𝗕𝘂𝘆, 𝘁𝗵𝗲𝗻 𝗽𝗹𝘂𝗴 𝗶𝗻

The twist: the team that built all this expects search to fall from 80% of new customers to 30%.

And on the AI search experts? "ChatGPT SEO gurus? It's b.s."

That's the real lesson. The keyword era built airSlate. The answer era rewards a different page: one AI quotes, not one that matches a string.

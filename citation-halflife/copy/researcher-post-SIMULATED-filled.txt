Half of this week's AI citations are gone in 11 days. Brand mentions last 5.8× longer.

We measured it: 14 weekly runs, 40,281 AI answers, 990 fixed buyer prompts, three consumer engines (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Mode). Then survival analysis on every cited URL, cited domain and named brand.

→ Half-life (median survival): URL 11d · domain 13d · brand 64d
→ Week-over-week turnover: 42% of cited URLs, 11% of brands
→ URL vs brand survival, log-rank: p < 0.001

Two things decay at very different rates. My hypothesis: they come from different parts of the system.

𝗧𝗵𝗲 𝗰𝗶𝘁𝗮𝘁𝗶𝗼𝗻 𝗰𝗼𝗺𝗲𝘀 𝗳𝗿𝗼𝗺 𝗿𝗲𝘁𝗿𝗶𝗲𝘃𝗮𝗹.
Query fan-out, a provider index, a reranker, dedup, then whatever fits a handful of slots. Every stage moves week to week. Rewrites drift, indexes refresh, near-duplicates swap places. A URL can drop out with nothing changing on the page.

𝗧𝗵𝗲 𝗯𝗿𝗮𝗻𝗱 𝗰𝗼𝗺𝗲𝘀 𝗺𝗼𝘀𝘁𝗹𝘆 𝗳𝗿𝗼𝗺 𝘁𝗵𝗲 𝗽𝗿𝗶𝗼𝗿.
The model's picture of a category, reinforced by thousands of documents naming the same leaders. Rotate the evidence and the conclusion barely moves. Same pattern as our 1,000-query study: engines shared under 14% of URLs but 35–42% of vendors.

𝗛𝗮𝘇𝗮𝗿𝗱 𝗶𝘀𝗻'𝘁 𝗳𝗹𝗮𝘁.
Risk of being dropped peaks in a citation's first week. A URL that survives 4 weeks has a 29% chance of lasting 4 more; a new one, 19%. Citations show infant mortality. Brands behave closer to Lindy.

𝗦𝗼𝘂𝗿𝗰𝗲 𝘁𝘆𝗽𝗲 𝗺𝗮𝘁𝘁𝗲𝗿𝘀 𝗺𝗼𝗿𝗲 𝘁𝗵𝗮𝗻 𝗲𝗻𝗴𝗶𝗻𝗲.
Vendor site 14d · review sites 12d · community/UGC 7d · media 10d. Across engines, URL half-life spans only 10 to 13 days.

𝗪𝗵𝗮𝘁 𝘁𝗵𝗶𝘀 𝗯𝗿𝗲𝗮𝗸𝘀:
1. Snapshot tracking. At 42% weekly turnover, one snapshot mostly measures sampling variance. Use rolling 4-week windows with intervals.
2. Attribution. "We won a citation" after a content change can't be told apart from turnover unless it outlives the base hazard.
3. The KPI. Mention rate is the stable estimand. Citations are noisy observations of it.

Caveats: one response per prompt per engine per week, so true decay and sampling variance aren't fully separable. Repeated within-week sampling is the next study. Spells longer than ~13 weeks are censored, so the brand half-life is a lower bound.

What this means:
→ There's no single "AI search". Your content is matched against four different query patterns.
→ Put the current year where retrieval can see it: title, H1, visible date, dateModified. Do it before 1 January.
→ Run site:yourdomain.com searches for your core questions. If the answer isn't on your own site, ChatGPT won't find it there.
→ Build prompt lists from more than one model's fan-out.

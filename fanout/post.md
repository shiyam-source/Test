## Post

When you ask an AI model a question, it rewrites it into searches first. Those are the fan-outs.

We decoded them across four models. They don't search the same way.

→ Claude Opus 5 puts a year in 57.4% of its searches. Haiku 4.5: 22.6%. It grows with model size.
→ ChatGPT uses site: in 20.4% of its searches to target one domain. It isn't in any Claude model's top 100.
→ "best" drops from 23.7% on Sonnet 5 to 3.3% on Opus 5. Opus drops the buyer phrasing.

What that means for your content:
→ Keep pages dated and fresh. Bigger models literally search for this year.
→ Be the official source for your own product. ChatGPT goes looking for it.
→ Don't bet everything on "best X" pages. The biggest model barely asks for them.

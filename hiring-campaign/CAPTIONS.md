# TripleDart hiring campaign: posting plan + captions

## Carousel or individual posts?

Go with the carousel, and follow it up with individual role posts.

**Why the carousel works:**
- Card 1 shows all 10 openings across 6 roles, so people can quickly see which one fits them and swipe to it.
- Carousels hold attention longer than single images, and both LinkedIn and Instagram reward that.
- One post that tells the whole story (every team, then why TripleDart) gets more shares than four posts that each tell part of it.

**The catch: animation in a carousel depends on the platform.**

| Platform | Format | Do the animations play? | Upload this |
|---|---|---|---|
| Instagram | Carousel (mixed photo + video, up to 20) | **Yes.** Each MP4 slide plays. | `output/mp4/01…06.mp4` |
| LinkedIn | Document (PDF) carousel | No, it's static, but it gets the most reach on LinkedIn | `output/tripledart-hiring-carousel.pdf` |
| LinkedIn | Multi-image post | GIFs usually stay static in multi-image posts, so test it first | (skip) |
| LinkedIn | Single GIF or video post | **Yes** | `output/gif/0X.gif` or `output/mp4/0X.mp4` |


**Suggested rollout** (LinkedIn posts at 7 PM IST, per your playbook):
1. **Day 1 (launch):** your personal post and the TripleDart page post, both with the PDF carousel, plus the Instagram MP4 carousel. JD links go in the **first comment**, never in the post body. On IG, put them in the link in bio or a Story link sticker.
2. **Day 2:** SEO and GEO team post (slide 05 as GIF/MP4).
3. **Day 4:** Paid Media and ABM team post (slide 04).
4. **Day 6:** Account Managers post (slide 03).
5. **Day 8:** Recruiter post (slide 02).
6. **Within 10 minutes of each post:** drop the link in #general with the comment starters at the bottom of this file. Early comments from the team decide reach.

LinkedIn rules from your playbook that this copy follows: no links in the body, at most 3 hashtags, no engagement bait, no two posts within 4 hours.

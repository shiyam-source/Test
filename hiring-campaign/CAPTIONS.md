# TripleDart hiring campaign: posting plan + captions

## Carousel or individual posts?

Go with the carousel, and follow it up with individual role posts.

**Why the carousel works:**
- Card 1 shows all three roles, so people can quickly see which one fits them and swipe to it.
- Carousels hold attention longer than single images, and both LinkedIn and Instagram reward that.
- One post that tells the whole story (the three roles, then why TripleDart) gets more shares than three posts that each tell part of it.

**The catch: animation in a carousel depends on the platform.**

| Platform | Format | Do the animations play? | Upload this |
|---|---|---|---|
| Instagram | Carousel (mixed photo + video, up to 20) | **Yes.** Each MP4 slide plays. | `output/mp4/01…05.mp4` |
| LinkedIn | Document (PDF) carousel | No, it's static, but it gets the most reach on LinkedIn | `output/tripledart-hiring-carousel.pdf` |
| LinkedIn | Multi-image post | GIFs usually stay static in multi-image posts, so test it first | (skip) |
| LinkedIn | Single GIF or video post | **Yes** | `output/gif/0X.gif` or `output/mp4/0X.mp4` |

**Suggested rollout:**
1. **Day 1 (launch):** LinkedIn PDF carousel + Instagram MP4 carousel. Put all 3 JD links in the LinkedIn caption, and the IG link in bio or a Story link sticker.
2. **Day 2:** Senior Recruiter as a single post (animated GIF/MP4), with its JD link.
3. **Day 4:** Account Managers as a single post.
4. **Day 6:** Director of Paid Media as a single post. Ask the leadership team to reshare this one.
5. **Every day:** the team reshares from their own profiles. Posts from people's own profiles usually reach more people than the company page does.

The single posts are where the GIFs do their job on LinkedIn. They also let each JD link sit on its own post instead of three links competing in one caption.

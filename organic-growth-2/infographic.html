05 · SURFACE BY SURFACE
Eight surfaces.
What to do on each one.
YOUR SITE

The one node you control

definition · comparison · pricing · integration pages
Data: a third of ChatGPT's links went to pricing pages. Definition pages are top-two for Perplexity, Claude and AI Overviews.
  • Public pricing with plan names in text
  • Honest "X vs Y" and "alternatives" pages
MEASURE pages cited per engine
AUDITS · SCHEMA · LINKSBRIEFS · DRAFTS
SEARCH ENGINES

Rank still feeds the answer

Google · Bing · Brave · DuckDuckGo
Data: Perplexity cited the #1 result 87% of the time, falling to 46% at #9. Rank was the only page signal that mattered.
  • Map each prompt to the query behind it
  • Top 3 on bottom-funnel terms; check Bing too
MEASURE position on prompt-mapped queries
RANK TRACKINGGAP RESEARCH · CTR
REFERENCE, NEWS & ROUNDUPS

Be in what they quote

Wikipedia · Reuters · WIRED · "best X" lists
Data: 74% of cited pages weren't in Google's top 10 or AI Overview. Most of what AI quotes is off your rank tracker.
  • Find and pitch the roundups engines cite
  • Publish original research worth citing
MEASURE cited third-party pages that name you
FIND + PITCHPRESS · WIKIPEDIA
COMMUNITIES & FORUMS

Huge for one engine. Zero for two.

Reddit · Quora · Stack Overflow
Data: Reddit was Perplexity's #1 cited domain. ChatGPT and Claude cited it 0 times in 1,000 answers.
  • Prioritise if Perplexity or AI Overviews matter
  • Answer real threads as a named person
MEASURE threads mentioning you vs competitors
MENTION TRACKINGPOSTING
REVIEW SITES

Proof in other people's words

G2 · Capterra · TrustRadius · Trustpilot
Why: buyers ask AI "is X any good?" and review sites are where that answer lives.
  • Ask for reviews right after a win, monthly
  • Mine review themes for comparison pages
MEASURE review count, rating, recency
G2 THEME MININGASKING CUSTOMERS

Answer the question on camera

YouTube · TikTok · Vimeo
Why: "how do I…" questions get answered with walkthroughs. Transcripts make video readable.
  • Film the 10 questions sales hears most
  • Turn every video into a written page
MEASURE videos covering your top prompts

Where your point of view spreads

LinkedIn · X · Instagram · Facebook
Why: treat social as distribution. Posts spread the research and point of view that engines do cite.
  • Every study becomes 5–10 posts
  • Point people back to the citable page
MEASURE branded search and mentions

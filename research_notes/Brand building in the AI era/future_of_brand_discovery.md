# Future of Brand Discovery for B2B SaaS in the AI Era (6 months / 1 year / 3 years from 2026-11-02)

Research date: 2026-11-02. Method note: 15 tool calls. Several primary domains (prnewswire.com, forrester.com, elenaverna.com) were blocked by the session's network allowlist, so many figures below come from search-result summaries of secondary sources. Primary verification is still needed before publishing. Items marked **[prior knowledge, not re-verified]** come from the researcher's pre-June-2026 training knowledge with a best-known URL. They were not fetched in this session.

## 1. Current trajectory data (adoption, zero-click, traffic, B2B buyer behavior)

### Takeaway
AI assistants are now the *starting point* for about half of B2B software buyers (G2, 2026), and ChatGPT has passed roughly 1B weekly users. Measured *aggregate* organic search traffic declines are still modest, though: about -2.5% YoY in US organic traffic as of Jan 2026, far short of Gartner's -25% call. The damage falls on click-through for informational queries, not on search usage as a whole. Discovery is moving faster than traffic is disappearing.

### Cited Findings
**ChatGPT scale**
- DATA: OpenAI announced >900M weekly active users on 2026-02-27, up from 400M a year earlier (+125% YoY). This is the best-corroborated figure. — [Superlines](https://www.superlines.io/articles/chatgpt-statistics/); [DemandSage](https://www.demandsage.com/chatgpt-statistics/)
- DATA (partially verified): An OpenAI statement of 2026-08-06, "Every week, 1 billion people turn to ChatGPT," is quoted by secondary sources. — [Wikipedia: ChatGPT](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ChatGPT); [Second Talent](https://www.secondtalent.com/resources/chatgpt-statistics/)
- DATA (unverified): 1.2B weekly users, reportedly stated at OpenAI DevDay on 2026-09-29. It appears only on secondary blogs and was not confirmed against OpenAI materials. — [TechnologyChecker](https://technologychecker.io/blog/chatgpt-statistics)
- DATA: About 50M paying consumer subscribers as of Feb 2026. — [Superlines](https://www.superlines.io/articles/chatgpt-statistics/)
- DATA: Ahrefs estimated ChatGPT query volume at roughly 12% of Google's as of Feb 2026, as cited by BrandGEO. This matters for scale: Google is still roughly 8x larger by query count. — [BrandGEO](https://brandgeo.co/blog/gartner-25-percent-search-drop-what-to-model)

**Google AI Overviews / AI Mode reach**
- DATA [prior knowledge, not re-verified]: Google said AI Overviews had more than 2B monthly users and AI Mode more than 100M monthly active users in the US and India (Alphabet Q2 2025 earnings, July 2025). — [Alphabet Q2 2025 earnings remarks](https://blog.google/inside-google/message-ceo/alphabet-earnings-q2-2025/)
- DATA (conflicting): Estimates of how many queries trigger AI Overviews range from about 13% to 48%, depending on sample and definition. — [The Stacc](https://thestacc.com/blog/google-ai-overview-statistics/); [Relevant Audience](https://www.relevantaudience.com/seo/ai-overview-impact-on-organic-search-2026/)
- GAP: No 2026 first-party Google figure for AI Mode users was retrieved.

**Zero-click / CTR**
- DATA: Seer Interactive's study of about 5.47M queries found organic CTR fell from 1.76% to 0.61% on queries showing AI Overviews. CTR also fell about 41% on queries *without* AI Overviews, which suggests a general drop in clicking. — [Dataslayer summary](https://www.dataslayer.ai/blog/google-ai-overviews-the-end-of-traditional-ctr-and-how-to-adapt-in-2025); [Relevant Audience](https://www.relevantaudience.com/seo/ai-overview-impact-on-organic-search-2026/)
- DATA: A Search Engine Journal field study found AI Overviews cut organic clicks by 38% on triggered queries. Sistrix (Germany) found an average click loss of 6.6% across all keywords. — [Relevant Audience](https://www.relevantaudience.com/seo/ai-overview-impact-on-organic-search-2026/)
- DATA [prior knowledge, not re-verified]: Pew Research (2025-07-22), based on browsing data from 900 US adults in March 2025, found users clicked a traditional result in 8% of visits with an AI summary versus 15% without one. They clicked a link inside the AI summary in only 1% of visits. — [Pew Research](https://www.pewresearch.org/short-reads/2025/07/22/google-users-are-less-likely-to-click-on-links-when-an-ai-summary-appears-in-the-results/)
- DATA (weak / flag as hype-prone): "93% zero-click in AI Mode" circulates widely, but its origin is a single vendor estimate and it is not independently verified. Other estimates put AI Overview zero-click at 72–83%. — [Pasquale Pillitteri](https://pasqualepillitteri.it/en/news/811/google-ai-mode-zero-click-seo-2026-en); [The Stacc](https://thestacc.com/blog/google-ai-overview-statistics/)

**Organic traffic decline**
- DATA: US organic search traffic fell 2.5% YoY as of Jan 2026 (Search Engine Land, cited via a secondary source). Individual sites and sectors report 40–70% losses. — [Relevant Audience / secondary](https://www.relevantaudience.com/seo/ai-overview-impact-on-organic-search-2026/); [Cintra](https://cintra.run/blog/organic-traffic-decline)
- INFERENCE-LEVEL: Losses are concentrated in top-of-funnel informational content (the "what is X" blog posts B2B SaaS relied on). The aggregate figure hides very uneven distribution.

**B2B buyers using AI to research vendors**
- DATA: G2's 2026 AI Search Insight Report (press release 2026-04-15; n=1,076 B2B software buyers) found:
  - 51% now *start* software research in an AI chatbot rather than a search engine, up from 29% in April 2025.
  - 71% rely on AI chatbots for software research, up from about 60% seven months earlier.
  - 69% chose a different vendor than initially planned because of chatbot guidance.
  - 33% bought from a vendor they were not previously familiar with.
  - 83% felt more confident in their choice.
  - The top use case was comparing vendor strengths and weaknesses (41%).
  - Caveat: G2 is a review marketplace with an incentive to frame AI as review-dependent. — [G2 report](https://learn.g2.com/g2-2026-ai-search-insight-report); [Morningstar/PRN](https://www.morningstar.com/news/pr-newswire/20260415cg34275/new-g2-research-half-of-b2b-software-buyers-now-start-their-research-with-ai-chatbots)
- DATA: TrustRadius (fielded January 2026, n=1,862 tech buyers) found 63% used AI during their purchase journey, and 94% of AI-using buyers fact-check its answers at least some of the time. — [Reechee summary](https://reechee.io/blog/state-of-b2b-software-buying/); [MarketScale](https://www.marketscale.com/industries/marketing-tech/ai-is-where-b2b-software-discovery-starts-in-2026-but-buyers-still-close-deals-on-demos-and-reviews)
- DATA: Wynter (January 2026, n=101 mid-market SaaS CMOs) found 84% use AI/LLMs for vendor discovery and 68% start research in AI tools before traditional search. Small sample. — [Reechee summary](https://reechee.io/blog/state-of-b2b-software-buying/)
- DATA (headline framing): "AI is where B2B software discovery starts in 2026, but buyers still close deals on demos and reviews." — [MarketScale](https://www.marketscale.com/industries/marketing-tech/ai-is-where-b2b-software-discovery-starts-in-2026-but-buyers-still-close-deals-on-demos-and-reviews)

### Inferences
- The G2 "33% bought from an unfamiliar vendor" and "69% switched" figures are the most important for a founder. AI is *redistributing* consideration, which means a smaller brand can get into a shortlist it would previously have missed. The opposite also holds: an incumbent can be dropped from a shortlist it once owned by default.
- The adoption curve (29% to 51% "start in AI" in about 12 months) suggests a majority-to-supermajority by late 2027 if the trend continues. Treat this as a projection, not data.
- Traffic is the wrong primary KPI. Influence on the shortlist is happening in places sites can't see, and that is the core "dark funnel" shift.

### Gaps
- No first-party Google 2026 figures on AI Mode usage were retrieved, and no first-party Perplexity 2026 usage figures.
- No 2026 Similarweb or SparkToro clickstream data on B2B site traffic was retrieved. The 6sense, Forrester buyer survey, and Responsive 2026 figures were not retrieved either.
- The primary G2 press release was blocked, so the survey field date (March vs April 2026) is unconfirmed.

## 2. Agents as buyers and researchers (ChatGPT agent, Atlas, Gemini, ACP/UCP, MCP): how brands get "chosen"

### Takeaway
Agentic *transactions* in B2B are real but narrow. They run through procurement suites (SAP Ariba/Joule, Coupa, Salesforce buyer agent) and are mostly for repeatable, low-risk buys. Agentic *research and shortlisting* is already mainstream through chat assistants. Agents pull from a third-party consensus layer: Reddit, LinkedIn, YouTube, Wikipedia, G2, and analyst/review content. A brand's own website is a much smaller input, and agent outputs are statistically noisy.

### Cited Findings
**Protocols / products**
- DATA: UCP (Universal Commerce Protocol) is Google's standard with Shopify, reportedly launched 2026-01-11. ACP (Agentic Commerce Protocol) comes from OpenAI and Stripe. Sources differ on scope: UCP is described as covering discovery through post-purchase, ACP as the checkout layer. One source wrongly attributes ACP to IBM/Linux Foundation, which conflicts with most other sources. — [Digital Applied](https://www.digitalapplied.com/blog/agentic-commerce-standards-ucp-acp-ap2-2026-merchant-guide); [Digital Applied protocol map](https://www.digitalapplied.com/blog/ai-agent-protocol-ecosystem-map-2026-mcp-a2a-acp-ucp); [Wikipedia: Agentic commerce](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agentic_commerce)
- DATA [prior knowledge, not re-verified]: OpenAI launched ChatGPT agent on 2025-07-17, announced ACP and Instant Checkout with Stripe on 2025-09-29, and launched the ChatGPT Atlas browser on 2025-10-21. Anthropic introduced MCP in November 2024. — [OpenAI: Introducing ChatGPT agent](https://openai.com/index/introducing-chatgpt-agent/); [OpenAI: Buy it in ChatGPT](https://openai.com/index/buy-it-in-chatgpt/); [OpenAI: Introducing ChatGPT Atlas](https://openai.com/index/introducing-chatgpt-atlas/); [Anthropic: MCP](https://www.anthropic.com/news/model-context-protocol)
- DATA (single vendor-focused source): "Your buyers' agents are more likely to reach you through Coupa, Ariba, or JAGGAER than through a chat window." Salesforce made a B2B buyer agent generally available in June 2026, and SAP shipped Joule agents across Ariba and Fieldglass in June 2026. — [BlueBolt](https://www.blueboltsolutions.com/agentic-commerce-b2b-manufacturing-2026/)

**Forecast (Forrester, 2026-10-21)**
- PREDICTION: In Forrester's 2027 B2B Go-To-Market Predictions, "AI agents will influence 75% of B2B purchase decisions" in 2027. Autonomous buying is expected to stay mostly in high-volume, low-risk, repeatable purchases. — [Forrester press release](https://www.forrester.com/press-newsroom/forresters-2027-b2b-go-to-market-predictions-ai-agents-will-influence-75-of-b2b-purchase-decisions/); [Forrester investor release](https://investor.forrester.com/news-releases/news-release-details/forresters-2027-b2b-go-market-predictions-ai-agents-will)
- PREDICTION: Buyers will *double* their human interactions to validate AI-generated recommendations, because AI cannot fully judge fit, feasibility, trust or accountability. They will turn to provider teams, analysts, references and partners. — [Forrester blog "AI Takes Over Business Buying, But Humans Retain Control"](https://www.forrester.com/blogs/predictions-2027-ai-takes-over-business-buying-but-humans-retain-control/); [FutureCIO](https://futurecio.tech/forrester-predicts-ai-agents-will-influence-75-of-b2b-purchase-decisions)
- UNCONFIRMED: "Two-thirds of providers will lose deals because buyers cannot verify their claims" appears only in one secondary summary. — [Rallies](https://rallies.ai/news/forrester-sees-ai-agents-influencing-75-of-b2b-purchase-decisions-in-2027-1986ed63f75ca462)

**What agents cite (the inputs to being "chosen")**
- DATA: Peec AI analyzed 30M sources and found Reddit is the #1 cited domain across ChatGPT, Google AI Mode, Gemini, Perplexity and AI Overviews, followed by YouTube and LinkedIn. — [Search Engine Land](https://searchengineland.com/ai-search-engines-cite-reddit-youtube-and-linkedin-most-study-473138)
- DATA: Ahrefs Brand Radar (July 2026) gives Reddit 16.7% mention share and Wikipedia 8.9%. Similarweb-based data puts Wikipedia at about 13% of ChatGPT citations. Rankings vary by dataset. — [SaaSy Links](https://saasylinks.com/blog/most-cited-websites-chatgpt/); [DemandNow](https://demandnow.ai/blog/most-cited-domains-chatgpt-may-2026/)
- DATA: SEMrush's 325K-prompt study found LinkedIn cited in 14.3% of ChatGPT Search responses and 5.3% of Perplexity responses. Profound reports LinkedIn rose to about #5 on ChatGPT by Feb 2026 and is the top domain for B2B/software queries. — [5W PR](https://www.5wpr.com/research/citation-source-audit-q1-2026/); [Contently](https://contently.com/2026/04/29/top-sources-llms-cite/)
- DATA: Perplexity's top cited sources include Reddit, LinkedIn, NIH, Microsoft and G2. In a small self-selected audit, G2 appeared in 66% of Perplexity audits versus 12% of ChatGPT audits. — [5W PR](https://www.5wpr.com/research/citation-source-audit-q1-2026/); [VisibAI](https://getvisibai.com/blog/which-sources-ai-assistants-cite-chatgpt-perplexity-2026)

**Noise / measurement**
- DATA: SparkToro (Rand Fishkin with Patrick O'Donnell of Gumshoe.ai; published about 2026-01-28) ran about 3,000 runs with roughly 600 volunteers, 12 prompts, and 3 platforms. There was less than a 1-in-100 chance of getting the same brand list twice from ChatGPT or Google AI, and roughly 1-in-1,000 for the same order. Fishkin called any tool offering "ranking position in AI" "full of baloney." Fishkin notes the study is small and not peer reviewed. — [Search Engine Journal](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/ai-recommendations-change-with-nearly-every-query-sparktoro/566242/); [MediaPost](https://www.mediapost.com/publications/article/412364/ai-brand-recommendations-chaotic-inconsistent.html)
- OPINION (secondary): The statistically meaningful metric is *visibility share*, meaning how often a brand appears across 60–100+ runs of a prompt set, not rank. — [Damesmarketing](https://www.damesmarketing.com/blog/ai-brand-visibility-noise-1-metric-that-isnt/); [Passionfruit](https://www.getpassionfruit.com/blog/why-ai-brand-recommendations-change-with-every-query-research-analysis-and-strategic-implications)

### Inferences
- An agent "chooses" a brand roughly in proportion to how often, how consistently and how credibly the brand appears across the corpus the model retrieves from. For B2B that corpus is Reddit threads, LinkedIn posts by real practitioners, YouTube, G2/review sites, Wikipedia, comparison/listicle pages and analyst content. This is earned media and third-party consensus, which is essentially PR plus community plus reviews. On-site schema is a hygiene factor, not a differentiator.
- Because outputs are probabilistic, brand presence works like *share of voice in a sampled distribution*. Being the default answer for a narrow, well-defined category ("best AI marketing agent for B2B SaaS") is more achievable than ranking for broad terms.
- In B2B, agentic *checkout* matters less than agentic *shortlisting*. Agents running inside procurement systems (Ariba, Coupa) will also want structured, verifiable data: security docs, pricing, integrations, compliance. That favors vendors with machine-readable trust artifacts (trust centers, public pricing, docs, MCP servers or APIs agents can query).
- Forrester's "double human interactions to validate" prediction implies the human trust layer becomes *more* valuable, not less. That layer is references, founder and employee credibility, analysts and partners.

### Gaps
- No primary data was found on how B2B buying agents actually build shortlists: ranking factors, or whether they query vendor MCP servers. This is an important unknown.
- Gemini agent / Google "agentic" B2B features, and Atlas adoption figures, were not retrieved.
- No study was found quantifying the effect of structured data or llms.txt on AI citation. Treat claims that these drive visibility as unproven.

## 3. Ads inside AI answers

### Takeaway
ChatGPT ads went from a US pilot (Feb 2026) to a self-serve platform (May 2026) to a reported $1B run rate (Aug 2026). Paid placement in AI surfaces is now a real channel. Ads are separated below the organic answer, which so far keeps organic AI visibility, and the earned consensus behind it, distinct and arguably more valuable.

### Cited Findings
- DATA: OpenAI began showing ads to logged-in adult Free and Go users in the US on 2026-02-09 and opened a self-serve ChatGPT Ads Manager to US businesses on 2026-05-05. Ads are bought on CPM/CPC through a relevance-weighted second-price auction with a $25/day minimum. — [Segwise](https://segwise.ai/blog/chatgpt-ads-2026-guide); [Launchcodex](https://launchcodex.com/blog/performance-marketing/chatgpt-ads-breakdown/); [Geotoolbox](https://geotoolbox.ai/blog/chatgpt-ads)
- DATA: Ads are labeled "Sponsored" and appear below the answer. OpenAI says ads do not change responses, advertisers cannot shape or rank responses, and advertisers get only aggregate data. — [Geotoolbox](https://geotoolbox.ai/blog/chatgpt-ads); [Wild Signal](https://wildsignal.agency/dispatches/brand-pr-ai-discovery/chatgpt-ads-are-here-what-brands-need-to-know)
- DATA: Formats expanded in March 2026. "Sponsored Agents," branded AI agents a user chats with after clicking an ad, are reported, with conflicting dates of 2026-07-31 and 2026-09-16. — [Prowlo](https://prowlo.com/blog/chatgpt-ads-ai-agents); [AI Tool Discovery](https://www.aitooldiscovery.com/guides/chatgpt-ads)
- DATA (single secondary source): OpenAI reported on 2026-08-31 that ChatGPT Ads hit a $1B annualized run rate in under 200 days. Early advertisers include Target, Ford, Adobe, Expedia, Best Buy, AT&T and Qualcomm. — [Segwise](https://segwise.ai/blog/chatgpt-ads-2026-guide)
- CONFLICT: Geographic reach is reported as 6 countries by one source and 40+ by another. One headline claims ads appear on 51% of US replies. — [Tech Insider](https://tech-insider.org/chatgpt-ads-rollout-2026/); [Segwise](https://segwise.ai/blog/chatgpt-ads-2026-guide)

### Inferences
- The search-engine history (organic results pushed below paid placements over about 15 years) is the base case. Expect ad load and ad formats in AI answers to grow through 2027–2029, and expect "Sponsored Agents" to blur the line between ads and answers. This is a prediction.
- For a B2B SaaS founder, AI ads are a *rentable* capture layer, much like Google Ads. The *compounding* asset is still organic inclusion in the answer itself, which is driven by third-party consensus. Paid can't buy inclusion in the answer text, at least for now, and that is what most buyers read first.
- Paid-tier users (about 50M subscribers) do not see ads, and they skew toward professionals and B2B buyers. Ad reach into B2B decision-makers may therefore be lower than headline user counts suggest. This is an inference.

### Gaps
- No primary OpenAI announcement or Help Center page was retrieved.
- No data on B2B advertiser performance or CPCs in ChatGPT.
- Google AI Mode / AI Overviews ad expansion in 2026 and Perplexity's ad status were not retrieved.

The best-known numeric forecast, Gartner's -25% search volume by 2026, has *not* materialized in aggregate data. That is a useful reminder to discount precise forecasts.

- OPINION [prior knowledge, not re-verified] (a16z, Zach Cohen and Seema Amble, May 2025, "How Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) Rewrites the Rules of Search"): The essay argues that the unit of competition shifts from ranking on a results page to being referenced inside the model's answer. Its often-quoted line is that "it's no longer just about click-through rates, it's about reference rates." Verbatim wording has not been verified. — [a16z](https://a16z.com/geo-over-seo/)
- PREDICTION [prior knowledge, not re-verified] (McKinsey, Aug 2025, "New front door to the internet"): About half of consumers already use AI-powered search, and McKinsey projects about $750B of US revenue flowing through AI-powered search by 2028. Figures are from memory; verify before use. — [McKinsey](https://www.mckinsey.com/capabilities/growth-marketing-and-sales/our-insights/new-front-door-to-the-internet-winning-in-the-age-of-ai-search)

- Forecast reliability: Gartner's 2024 call overstated the *speed* of aggregate search decline but was directionally right about the shift in *where research begins* (G2: 51% start in AI). Use forecasts for direction, not magnitude.

## 5. Countertrends: IRL, community, human-verified content, creators, employee and founder-led brand

### Takeaway
Budgets are moving toward people-led and human-verified channels: B2B creators/influencers, events, and employee/founder content on LinkedIn. These channels both build direct trust and feed the sources AI cites most (LinkedIn, YouTube, Reddit). The countertrend and the AI-visibility strategy are largely the *same* strategy.

### Cited Findings
- DATA: Influencer Marketing Hub (600+ marketers) found about 87% expect bigger influencer budgets in 2026. Aspire found 74% plan to raise spend. Linqia's enterprise survey found 62% increasing. These are vendor surveys. — [Webtonic](https://www.webtonic.io/blog/influencer-marketing-statistics); [Digital Applied](https://www.digitalapplied.com/blog/influencer-marketing-statistics-2026-data-points)
- DATA: TopRank/Ascend2 found 53% of B2B influencer budgets grew in 2025. In-person events ranked as the second most effective B2B influencer format (39%) after social posts (56%). — [ContentGrip](https://www.contentgrip.com/influencer-marketing-statistics/); [Konabayev](https://konabayev.com/blog/influencer-marketing-b2b-2026/)
- DATA: Favikon's survey of 125 B2B creators found event speaking commands about $1,500, roughly 4x a sponsored LinkedIn post. — [Favikon](https://www.favikon.com/blog/the-complete-b2b-influencer-pricing-guide-2026-benchmarks-geography-industry-format)
- PREDICTION: Forrester expects 75% of enterprise B2B firms to raise influencer-relations budgets in 2026. — [The Ad Spend](https://theadspend.com/blog/b2b-influencer-creator-marketing-playbook)
- DATA: LinkedIn is the top cited domain for B2B/software queries in ChatGPT (Profound, Feb 2026), and Reddit/YouTube/LinkedIn lead AI citations overall. Employee, founder and creator content on these platforms therefore directly feeds AI answers. — [Contently](https://contently.com/2026/04/29/top-sources-llms-cite/); [Search Engine Land](https://searchengineland.com/ai-search-engines-cite-reddit-youtube-and-linkedin-most-study-473138)
- DATA: 94% of AI-using tech buyers fact-check AI answers (TrustRadius, Jan 2026). Buyers "still close deals on demos and reviews." — [MarketScale](https://www.marketscale.com/industries/marketing-tech/ai-is-where-b2b-software-discovery-starts-in-2026-but-buyers-still-close-deals-on-demos-and-reviews)
- OPINION: Elena Verna argues "AI confidence theater" is eroding trust in every brand. — [The State of Brand](https://www.thestateofbrand.com/news/elena-verna-ai-confidence-theater-eroding-trust)

### Inferences
- Large volumes of AI-generated content devalue anonymous brand content, and named humans with track records gain value. Founder-led and employee-led LinkedIn presence, podcast and YouTube appearances, and creator partnerships work twice: once for human trust and again as input to AI citations.
- IRL events generate unscripted proof (talks, recordings, photos, social posts) and relationships, which become the "human validation" Forrester expects buyers to seek more of.

### Gaps
- No hard 2026 data was found on B2B event attendance or budget recovery, or on community-led growth spend.
- No data was found on content-provenance standards (e.g., C2PA "human-verified" labels) affecting B2B trust.
- LinkedIn's own 2026 B2B creator or video data was not retrieved.

## 6. What will likely NOT change, what is probably hype, and scenarios with signposts (6 months / 1 year / 3 years)

### Takeaway
The fundamentals still hold: buyers buy from vendors they have heard of and trust, word of mouth and peer proof, category ownership, and product quality and customer outcomes. AI makes them *more* legible to machines. Hype concentrates in deterministic "AI rank" claims, tactical GEO hacks, near-term autonomous B2B purchasing, and precise traffic-apocalypse numbers.

### Cited Findings (grounding for the fundamentals and hype calls)
- Buyers still validate with humans, demos and reviews. — [Forrester](https://www.forrester.com/blogs/predictions-2027-ai-takes-over-business-buying-but-humans-retain-control/); [MarketScale](https://www.marketscale.com/industries/marketing-tech/ai-is-where-b2b-software-discovery-starts-in-2026-but-buyers-still-close-deals-on-demos-and-reviews)
- AI rank positions are statistically noisy, so "rank #1 in ChatGPT" offers are suspect. — [Search Engine Journal / SparkToro](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/ai-recommendations-change-with-nearly-every-query-sparktoro/566242/)
- Autonomous agent buying stays mostly in high-volume, low-risk categories (Forrester 2027). — [Forrester](https://www.forrester.com/press-newsroom/forresters-2027-b2b-go-to-market-predictions-ai-agents-will-influence-75-of-b2b-purchase-decisions/)
- The 25%-by-2026 search drop did not materialize in aggregate. — [WiloAI](https://wiloai.com/gartner-predicts-25-drop-in-search-volume-by-2026-what-this-means-for-yourbusiness/)
- Unverified dramatic stats ("93% zero-click in AI Mode") circulate from vendor blogs. — [Pasquale Pillitteri](https://pasqualepillitteri.it/en/news/811/google-ai-mode-zero-click-seo-2026-en)

### Inferences

**Likely NOT to change (bet on these)**
1. Mental availability and familiarity drive shortlists. AI amplifies brands that are already widely and consistently discussed, because the model's "consensus" is aggregated human discussion.
2. Third-party proof beats self-claims: reviews, case studies, references, analysts, and customers talking in public.
3. Clear category positioning. A model can only recommend you for a job it can associate you with in plain language.
4. Trust and accountability in high-stakes B2B purchases need humans (Forrester).
5. Owned audience (email list, community, founder following) is the only distribution not rented from Google, OpenAI or LinkedIn.

**Probably hype or over-claimed (as of Nov 2026)**
- "Rank #1 in ChatGPT" guarantees, and single-run AI rank trackers.
- llms.txt or schema as a primary AI-visibility lever. No evidence found.
- Fully autonomous agents buying mid-market and enterprise SaaS end to end by 2027.
- "SEO is dead." Google is still roughly 8x ChatGPT by query volume (Ahrefs, Feb 2026), and AI systems themselves retrieve from search indexes.
- Mass-produced AI content as a GEO strategy. It erodes trust, and citations favor Reddit, LinkedIn and YouTube human content.

**Scenarios and signposts**

*Horizon 1: about 6 months (May 2027)*
- Base case: "Start research in AI" passes about 60% of B2B software buyers (extrapolating G2's 29% to 51% over about 12 months). ChatGPT ads expand internationally and add formats. GEO measurement consolidates around share-of-voice across prompt sets. Forrester-style "human validation" shows up as more reference and peer requests.
- Signposts to watch:
  - G2's or TrustRadius's next survey wave and its "start in AI" percentage.
  - OpenAI disclosures of ad load, B2B ad formats, and whether ads appear for paid tiers.
  - Whether Sponsored Agents become broadly available, which would blur ads and answers.
  - Google moving AI Mode toward default, or putting ads in AI Mode.
  - Citation share shifts between Reddit and LinkedIn, and any platform licensing changes.

*Horizon 2: about 1 year (late 2027)*
- Base case: Forrester's "agents influence 75% of B2B purchases" is broadly realized for research and shortlisting. Procurement agents (Ariba/Joule, Coupa, Salesforce) handle renewals and commodity SaaS. Vendors expose machine-readable trust data (pricing, security, integrations, MCP endpoints). Paid AI placements become a standard line item next to Google Ads.
- Upside scenario for challengers: AI redistribution persists (G2's 33% buying from unfamiliar vendors), so focused category leaders with strong third-party consensus take share from incumbents.
- Downside scenario: Ad load rises and answers become pay-to-play, as search did, so organic AI visibility erodes and owned audience plus human channels carry more weight.
- Signposts to watch:
  - Measured aggregate Google query volume (does it finally decline?).
  - Procurement-agent adoption numbers.
  - Whether LLM vendors let agents query vendor MCP/APIs directly during shortlisting.
  - Regulatory moves on ad disclosure in AI answers.

*Horizon 3: about 3 years (2029)*
- Base case (prediction, low confidence on magnitude): Most B2B discovery and first-pass evaluation is AI-mediated, and website traffic stops being the core top-of-funnel metric. Brand becomes "how the models and the humans describe you": a reputation graph built from reviews, community discussion, creator and analyst coverage, and customer outcomes. Human-led channels (events, founder and creator media, communities) are the premium trust layer. Brands with big owned audiences and clear category ownership compound, while brands that relied on SEO content farms fade.
- Wildcards:
  - Model providers weight "verified" or licensed sources, which would favor publishers and review platforms with deals.
  - Agent-to-agent negotiation in procurement becomes standard.
  - A trust backlash against AI recommendations triggered by ads or manipulation scandals.
- Signposts to watch:
  - Share of B2B deals with an AI agent in the loop (Forrester/Gartner trackers).
  - Model providers' policies on sponsored content in answers.
  - Content-licensing deals (Reddit and others) that shape what models cite.
  - Whether "visibility share" becomes a standard reported metric in analytics suites (e.g., HubSpot AEO tools, GA).

**Implications for the founder (TripleDart + Slate)**: These are inferences, not data.
- Invest in assets that compound across both human and AI channels:
  - Founder and employee LinkedIn presence.
  - A recurring media property (podcast/YouTube).
  - Customer reviews on G2.
  - Authentic Reddit and community presence.
  - Original research or data that others cite. AI and journalists both reward primary data.
  - Selective IRL events.
- Own a crisp category phrase for Slate (e.g., "AI marketing agent for B2B SaaS") and get third parties to repeat it. Measure visibility share across a stable prompt set over many runs, not rank.
- Treat ChatGPT ads as a rentable test channel, not the brand strategy.

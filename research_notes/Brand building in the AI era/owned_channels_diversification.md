# Owned and Alternative Channels for a B2B SaaS Founder Brand (2025–2026)

Research context: written 2026-11-02 for Shiyam Sunder (TripleDart / Slate). Audience: B2B SaaS marketers, CMOs, founders, agencies.

Method note: searches covered all the key questions. Full-page fetches were blocked by the session's network allowlist for beehiiv.com, tryprofound.com, searchengineland.com and podnews.net. As a result, many figures below come from search-result summaries of those pages, plus secondary aggregators, and are flagged that way. Treat vendor and aggregator numbers as directional. Date every figure as given.

## 1. Substack in 2025–2026: growth, network discovery, B2B case studies, AI saturation

### Takeaway
Substack is the strongest diversification bet for a B2B founder writing long-form. It has about 5M paid subscriptions (since March 2025), a $1.1B valuation (July 2025), and audience growth in 2026. Most importantly, it has a working in-network discovery engine: Recommendations drove 50–78% of new free subscribers for top B2B writers such as Lenny Rachitsky and Kyle Poyar. The tech category is also the most AI-saturated on the platform, with 28% of top tech-newsletter writing flagged as fully or partially AI-generated in April 2026. Original, data-backed, first-person operator content is therefore the way to stand out.

### Cited Findings
**Platform scale and funding**
- Substack raised a $100M Series C led by BOND and The Chernin Group (TCG) in July 2025, at a reported $1.1B valuation (about 70% above its 2021 valuation of $650M). It said the money would go to "doubling down on the Substack app" and the network's social features. — [American Bazaar, 2025-07-18](https://americanbazaaronline.com/2025/07/18/substack-raises-100-million-in-series-c-to-grow-creator-subscription-platform-465236/); [BetaKit](https://betakit.com/canadian-co-founded-substack-raises-100-million-usd-series-c-to-grow-tools-for-independent-creators/)
- Paid subscriptions passed 5 million (about March 2025), up from 2 million in 2023 and 4 million in November 2024. — [BetaKit](https://betakit.com/canadian-co-founded-substack-raises-100-million-usd-series-c-to-grow-tools-for-independent-creators/); [Search Engine Land Substack stats guide](https://searchengineland.com/guide/substack-users). One aggregator cites "4.0 million active paid" attributed to Press Gazette: [VoxBooster](https://voxbooster.com/blog/substack-statistics-2026/). The 5M figure is better sourced.
- A May 2026 Hollywood Reporter piece (via aggregators) says more than 5M paid subscriptions and more than 50 creators earning over $1M a year. — [Search Engine Land guide](https://searchengineland.com/guide/substack-users); [DemandSage](https://www.demandsage.com/substack-statistics/)
- About 100,000 publications earned money on Substack as of April 2026, up from about 50,000 in May 2025 (aggregator claim). — [Social Day](https://socialday.live/features/substack-hits-100-000-paid-publications-as-creators-use-it-as-the-loyalty-layer-)
- Press Gazette UK data: Substack had the largest UK audience increase in its ranking, up 38.1% year on year to 4.2M. Time spent in January 2026 was up 112% year on year to 54.7M minutes. In another month it was still growing 75% year on year. — [Press Gazette UK top-50 ranking](https://pressgazette.co.uk/media-audience-and-business-data/media_metrics/most-popular-websites-news-uk-monthly-2)
- Paid Substack subscriptions to UK-based creators passed half a million (2026, Hollywood Reporter exclusive via Yahoo Finance). — [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/paid-substack-subscriptions-u-k-100000875.html)

**Video, live and app**
- January 2026: Substack launched Substack TV (beta), a standalone Apple TV and Google TV app for creators' video posts and livestreams. It is open to free and paid subscribers. — [Wikipedia: Substack](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Substack)

**Network-driven growth (the key mechanic)**
- "Over 30% of paid Substack subscriptions come from within its network" (Substack 2026 figure, quoted by an aggregator; primary page not retrieved). — [MangoThrive](https://mangothrive.com/substack-statistics/)
- Lenny's Newsletter: Substack Recommendations from other newsletters brought in 78% of new free readers and 12% of new paying ones. Lenny estimates he would be at about 500K subscribers without them. (Secondary teardown; the period measured is unclear and probably 2023–2024.) — [Lenny's Newsletter review/teardown summary via search](https://hark.news/newsletters/lennys-newsletter-review); [Growth in Reverse](https://growthinreverse.substack.com/p/the-5-biggest-takeaways-from-lennys)
- Lenny's growth: about 500K subscribers (September 2023, credited to Recommendations plus guest posts on First Round Review), 1M (March 2025), and 1.2M+ (April 2026, homepage header). Estimated "tens of thousands" paying; one review estimates about 18K. — [Lenny's Newsletter homepage](https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/); [Growth in Reverse](https://growthinreverse.substack.com/p/the-5-biggest-takeaways-from-lennys); [Josh Masters](https://joshmasters.substack.com/p/how-lenny-rachitsky-built-the-biggest)
- Kyle Poyar's Growth Unhinged had 78,000+ subscribers and 4M annual views (October 2025, when Kyle went full-time on the newsletter) and about 85–86K on its homepage later. Kyle said "about half of my new subscribers came that way" (Recommendations), with "over 500 publications" recommending him. — [A new chapter, Growth Unhinged](https://kylepoyar.substack.com/p/a-new-chapter); [Growth Unhinged homepage](https://kylepoyar.substack.com/); [Thormeier interview](https://blog.thormeier.co/p/how-to-build-and-monetize-a-newsletter)
- The Pragmatic Engineer (Gergely Orosz) passed 1M subscribers in April 2025. Orosz describes the growth as organic, with readers concentrated in engineering and engineering leadership. — [More than one million readers, Pragmatic Engineer](https://newsletter.pragmaticengineer.com/p/one-million)

**AI-content crowding**
- Late 2024: GPTZero (with WIRED) scanned the top 100 Substacks. About 10% used AI in some form and 7% relied on it heavily, mostly finance and investing newsletters. — [GPTZero](https://gptzero.me/news/substack-human-vs-ai-writers/); [eWeek](https://www.eweek.com/news/top-substack-newsletters-use-ai-tech/)
- April 2026: Taylor Lorenz and Pangram analyzed thousands of posts from bestseller lists. 28% of writing in top Technology newsletters was fully or partially AI-generated, the highest of any category. — [search summary of Lorenz/Pangram study; see NPR coverage](https://www.npr.org/2026/08/12/nx-s1-5919251/substacks-new-ai-detection-feature-has-divided-its-community)
- August 2026: Substack launched a reader-facing AI-detection label built with Pangram. It shows an estimated share of AI-written text and has split the community over false-positive fears. — [NPR, 2026-08-12](https://www.npr.org/2026/08/12/nx-s1-5919251/substacks-new-ai-detection-feature-has-divided-its-community); [Quasa](https://quasa.io/media/substack-now-lets-readers-check-if-a-newsletter-was-written-by-ai)

### Inferences
- For a B2B founder, Substack's compounding asset is the Recommendations graph. The playbook the top B2B writers used is to get recommended by adjacent writers (Growth Unhinged, Elena's Growth Scoop, Marketing Ideas and similar), guest-post or co-author, and recommend others back. Notes and the app add in-network discovery that beehiiv and Kit do not have natively.
- Substack now publicly labels AI-written text, and the tech category is the most saturated. A brand that markets an AI product (Slate) gains credibility by visibly writing in a human voice, with proprietary data such as TripleDart client benchmarks and Slate usage data. It loses credibility with generic AI explainers.
- Substack TV and livestreams mean a founder's video podcast or live AMAs can live inside the same subscriber relationship.

### Gaps
- No primary Substack disclosure was found on Notes usage, app MAU, or the exact current split of network versus external subscriber acquisition. Aggregator figures (for example, "32M new subscribers from in-app discovery") are unsourced and were excluded.
- No verified subscriber or growth data was found for Kevin Indig's Growth Memo, Elena Verna, or Emily Kramer (MKT1).
- Lenny's 78% figure is dated and secondhand.

## 2. Medium in 2025–2026: honest verdict

### Takeaway
Medium has stabilized financially: profitable since August 2024, with more than 1M paying members as of 2024. But it is a weak channel for a B2B founder brand. It has no email ownership, it is hostile to AI-assisted text (undisclosed AI gets follower-only reach and AI text cannot be paywalled), it has no B2B buyer network effect, and creators report falling views. Its main residual value is as an AI-citation surface (it shows up in Gemini and AI Overviews data). Cross-post canonical pieces there; do not build on it.

### Cited Findings
- Medium had its first profitable month in August 2024, after losing about $2.6M a month when Tony Stubblebine took over in 2022. It reported more than 1M paid subscribers as of April 2024. — [TechCrunch, 2024-08-21](https://techcrunch.com/2024/08/21/august-was-mediums-first-profitable-month-ever); [TechCrunch, 2025-07-11](https://techcrunch.com/2025/07/11/mediums-ceo-explains-what-it-took-to-stop-losing-2-6m-monthly); [Nieman Lab](https://www.niemanlab.org/reading/fell-in-a-hole-got-out-how-medium-returned-to-profitability-after-a-near-death-experience/)
- In mid-2025 Stubblebine said the company remains profitable. — [Fast Company](https://www.fastcompany.com/91407562/medium-ceo-tony-stubblebine-interview); [Kottke, July 2025](https://kottke.org/25/07/0047147-ceo-tony-stubblebine-shar)
- AI policy (effective 2024-05-01, still in force per 2026 guides): AI-generated writing cannot be paywalled in the Partner Program. Disclosed AI writing gets General Distribution but not editor "Boost". Undisclosed AI writing is limited to "Network Only" (followers). — [BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/technology/medium-bans-ai-generated-content-from-its-paid-partner-program/); [Plagiarism Today, 2024-04-11](https://www.plagiarismtoday.com/2024/04/11/medium-sets-new-policies-on-ai-generated-writing/); [CMO Owls 2026 guide](https://www.cmoowls.com/blog/how-to-write-for-medium/)
- Stubblebine said Medium got "ten times busier" with AI submissions and that "most of it was slop". — [IBTimes](https://www.ibtimes.com/medium-ceo-tony-stubblebine-why-ai-could-kill-creator-economy-3806165); [Semafor, 2024-02-07](https://www.semafor.com/article/02/07/2024/what-mediums-tony-stubblebine-has-learned-about-tech-and-journalism)
- Creator sentiment is negative (opinion, not data): "Medium is dead as a doornail despite…", and writers complain of falling views. — [The Writing Long Game](https://thewritinglonggame.substack.com/p/medium-is-dead-as-a-doornail-despite); [Linda C.](https://lindac.substack.com/p/if-your-medium-views-are-down-i-have); [Is Medium Dying?](https://agileway.substack.com/p/is-medium-dying)
- Medium appears among cited domains for Gemini and Google AI Overviews in Peec AI's 30M-source analysis (March 2026). — [Peec AI](https://peec.ai/blog/top-domains-cited-by-ai-search-analysis-based-on-30m-sources)

### Inferences
- Verdict: low priority. If you use it, republish Substack or blog essays to a Medium publication with a canonical link, purely for AI and search surface area. Expect little B2B buyer engagement.

### Gaps
- No Similarweb or other 2025–2026 traffic figures for medium.com were found.
- No data on what share of Medium's readers are B2B decision-makers.

## 3. Alternatives: beehiiv/Kit, YouTube, podcasts, X, Reddit, Threads/Bluesky, communities, IRL

### Takeaway
After Substack, the best returns for this audience are likely to come from:
- a video podcast distributed on YouTube, the number one US podcast platform with 1B+ monthly podcast viewers as of January 2025;
- a selective Reddit presence, for AI citations and practitioner trust;
- curated IRL dinners.

X still has scale but is shrinking on mobile. Threads is growing but not B2B-dense. Bluesky is in decline. beehiiv and Kit beat Substack on ownership and monetization tooling but lack Substack's discovery network.

### Cited Findings
**Owned-newsletter platforms (beehiiv, Kit)**

Average click-through rate 3.23%, down from 4.74%. beehiiv itself notes that Apple Mail Privacy Protection inflates opens. — [beehiiv, State of Newsletters 2026](https://www.beehiiv.com/blog/the-state-of-newsletters-2026) (figures from a search summary; page fetch blocked); [Shno newsletter statistics](https://www.shno.co/marketing-statistics/newsletter-statistics)

**Communities and IRL**
- 2026 event listings show a shift to curated, application-only or invite-only founder dinners, such as stage-specific dinners in NYC and invite-only Founder Dinners at Startup Boston Week (2026-09-14 to 09-18) built around "candid conversations rather than panels". — [NYC B2B guide, June 2026](https://nycb2b.org/blog/nyc-startup-events-june-2026); [Startup Boston Week 2026](https://www.startupbos.org/sbw2026/side-events)

### Inferences
- **beehiiv/Kit vs. Substack:** pick Substack if network discovery is the goal, which it is for a brand still building. beehiiv offers a referral program, ad network, custom domain/SEO and data export, and suits a company-branded newsletter (for example, a Slate or TripleDart publication). A common hybrid is a personal Substack plus a company newsletter on beehiiv or HubSpot.
- **YouTube:** the B2B consumption gap (50% of marketers publishing vs. 34% of professionals consuming) argues against generic B2B YouTube. A founder-hosted video podcast interviewing CMOs is different. The guest becomes a distribution partner, and the footage feeds Shorts, LinkedIn clips and Substack posts. Shorts on their own are discovery with weak B2B intent.
- **X:** still valuable for AI, dev-tool and VC audiences. Less so for B2B SaaS marketers and CMOs, who have largely consolidated on LinkedIn.
- **Threads:** growth is consumer-led. **Bluesky:** skip.
- **Communities:** Pavilion, RevGenius and Slack groups suit relationship-building and seeding content, not broadcasting.

## 4. Owned vs. rented audience: open rates, deliverability, and evidence

### Takeaway
The case for owning the audience strengthened in 2025–2026. Third-party trackers estimate LinkedIn creator views fell about 47% and company-page reach fell 60–66% between 2024 and early 2026. Over the same period, newsletter open rates on beehiiv rose (41% in 2025). Email ownership now carries technical obligations: Gmail and Yahoo since February 2024, and Microsoft since 2025-05-05, require authentication and easy unsubscribe from bulk senders.

### Cited Findings
- Richard van der Blom's Algorithm InSights (1.8M+ posts): LinkedIn views down about 47%, engagement down about 39%, follower growth down about 42% (2025 versus the prior year, per secondary summaries). — [LinkedIn Organic Reach 2026, Updately](https://updately.ai/blog/linkedin-organic-reach-drop-pipeline-2026); [Contentin LinkedIn stats, September 2026](https://contentin.io/blog/linkedin-content-statistics/)
- Company-page organic reach fell 60–66% from 2024 to early 2026 (Ordinal/AuthoredUp data). One estimate puts company-page reach at about 2%, down from 7%. LinkedIn does not publish reach data, so all figures are third-party. — [Linkmagnet](https://linkmagnet.gg/blog/statistiques-portee-organique-linkedin-2026); [Yepads](https://yepads.com/linkedin-algorithm-changes-2026-why-linkedin-reach-is-dropping/)
- Newsletter open rates rose on beehiiv: 37.98% (2024) to 41.24% (2025). Clicks fell from 4.74% to 3.23%. — [beehiiv, State of Newsletters 2026](https://www.beehiiv.com/blog/the-state-of-newsletters-2026)
- Microsoft (Outlook, Hotmail, Live) began enforcing bulk-sender rules on 2025-05-05 for senders of more than 5,000 messages a day. The rules require SPF, DKIM, DMARC (at least p=none, aligned) and a working unsubscribe. Non-compliant mail initially went to Junk. A July 2026 guide reports permanent rejections (550 5.7.515), but the date of that change is unverified. — [Spam Resource, April 2025](https://www.spamresource.com/2025/04/microsoft-joins-club-top-four-b2c-mbps.html); [Mailgun](https://www.mailgun.com/blog/deliverability/microsoft-sender-requirements/); [Customer.io](https://customer.io/learn/deliverability/microsoft-teams-up-with-google-and-yahoo-on-email)
- Gmail and Yahoo rules (February 2024) also require one-click unsubscribe and low spam-complaint rates (under 0.3%). — [Warmy](https://www.warmy.io/guides/google-yahoo-sender-requirements/); [FXRM, two years on](https://fxrm.com/blog/google-and-yahoos-2024-bulk-sender-rules-two-years-on-what-actually-got-enforced)
- Lenny shows the owned asset compounding into a business: 1.2M+ subscribers, a paid tier, a 30K+ member paid Slack community, and a podcast. — [Lenny's Newsletter](https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/); [The Rebooting, "The rise of Lenny"](https://www.therebooting.com/p/the-rise-of-lenny)

### Inferences
- A founder brand that lives only on LinkedIn has single-algorithm risk; LinkedIn's 360Brew-era changes show the downside. Funnel LinkedIn reach into a newsletter, with a CTA in the profile, Featured section and comments. Each subscriber is then a reachable asset that survives algorithm changes.
- Opens are an inflated metric because of Apple MPP. Track clicks, replies and demo or pipeline attribution instead.
- On Substack or beehiiv, deliverability compliance is handled by the platform. A self-hosted list (HubSpot and similar) needs DMARC alignment and one-click unsubscribe.

### Gaps
- No rigorous study was found that directly compares the pipeline value of an email subscriber with a LinkedIn follower for B2B.
- No independent (non-vendor) 2025–2026 B2B newsletter open-rate benchmark was found.

## 5. Which channels feed AI search and LLM answers

### Takeaway
Across several 2026 studies, Reddit, YouTube and LinkedIn are the most-cited user-generated domains in AI answers. The split depends on the engine:
- **ChatGPT:** Reddit and Wikipedia lead, though Reddit's share there is volatile.
- **Perplexity and Google AI Overviews:** YouTube leads.
- **Medium:** appears in Gemini and AI Overviews data.
- **Substack:** no reliable figures.

For B2B software buying queries specifically, one study found Reddit almost absent. Vendor comparison pages and independent editorial content dominate there.

### Cited Findings
- Peec AI (30M sources, March 2026): Reddit is the top cited source across ChatGPT, Google AI Mode, Gemini, Perplexity and AI Overviews. YouTube, LinkedIn, Wikipedia and Forbes are also in the top five. Medium appears for Gemini and AI Overviews. — [Peec AI](https://peec.ai/blog/top-domains-cited-by-ai-search-analysis-based-on-30m-sources)
- A Search Engine Land study (2026) found AI search engines cite Reddit, YouTube and LinkedIn most. — [Search Engine Land](https://searchengineland.com/ai-search-engines-cite-reddit-youtube-and-linkedin-most-study-473138)
- Ahrefs Brand Radar (as reported): Reddit leads ChatGPT at 16.7%. YouTube leads Perplexity at 31.2% and AI Overviews at 21.1%. YouTube barely registers in ChatGPT. — [Contently, 2026-04-29](https://contently.com/2026/04/29/top-sources-llms-cite/); [LLM Pulse](https://llmpulse.ai/data-studies/top-cited-domains)
- Similarweb (about 600K US ChatGPT citations, January–February 2026): Wikipedia 13.15%, Reddit 11.97%. Ahrefs reports Reddit overtook Wikipedia in ChatGPT by July 2026. — [Saasylinks summary of 7 studies](https://saasylinks.com/blog/most-cited-websites-chatgpt/); [Trendly](https://blog.trendlyai.com/most-cited-domains-chatgpt)
- Volatility: Reddit's ChatGPT citation share dropped sharply in September 2025. Promptwatch recorded a fall from 3.8% to 0.5% in mid-August 2026, flagged as provisional. — [Omnibound](https://www.omnibound.ai/blog/reddit-data-b2b-ai-search)
- B2B software counter-evidence: in 1,739 ChatGPT and Perplexity citations for B2B software queries, Reddit appeared 11 times (under 1%, all from Perplexity). Independent content and vendor comparison pages made up 79%. — [Omnibound / B2B citation study summary](https://www.omnibound.ai/blog/reddit-data-b2b-ai-search); [BeVisibleIQ B2B SaaS AI citation study](https://bevisibleiq.com/ai-citations-study-2026/)
- Tinuiti Q1 2026: Reddit holds about 40% of consolidated AI citation share among social and UGC sources, and 24% of Perplexity's January 2026 citations. — [Growfusely AI search stats](https://growfusely.com/blog/ai-search-statistics/)
- YouTube reportedly overtook Reddit as the number one social source for AI citations in early 2026 (Adweek and others, via aggregator). — [Everything-PR citation index](https://everything-pr.com/ai-platform-citation-source-index-2026)
- Averi's B2B SaaS benchmark puts YouTube at 23.3% of Google AI Overviews citations (methodology unclear). — [Averi](https://www.averi.ai/how-to/chatgpt-vs.-perplexity-vs.-google-ai-mode-the-b2b-saas-citation-benchmarks-report-(2026))

### Inferences
- **YouTube** (transcripts are indexed) is the most reliable cross-engine citation surface for a founder. A video podcast pulls double duty for audience and for AI visibility.
- **LinkedIn** is a top-five cited domain in several studies, so it is already contributing, especially long-form posts and articles.
- **Reddit** helps on Perplexity and general queries. Its value for B2B buying queries is contested. Authentic participation in r/SaaS, r/marketing and r/B2BMarketing is a hedge, not a core channel. Overt promotion risks bans and backlash.
- For "best GTM agency" or "AI marketing agent" queries, the evidence says the bigger lever is third-party comparison and listicle pages, plus your own comparison pages. This is outside this channel scope but relevant.

### Gaps
- No reliable data on Substack's AI citation share. Substack posts are on public, indexable URLs, but no study quantified them.
- The study methodologies differ (share of citations vs. mention presence) and many are vendor blogs. The Profound and Search Engine Land primary pages could not be fetched.

## 6. Effort vs. return: cadence, repurposing workflows, time cost

### Takeaway
The successful B2B newsletters were built on one consistent weekly anchor plus network effects. Kyle Poyar reached 78K subscribers and 4M annual views on a weekly cadence before going full-time in October 2025, and Lenny used guest posts and Recommendations. The efficient stack for a busy founder is one anchor (a weekly or biweekly Substack essay, or a video podcast episode) atomized into LinkedIn, Shorts, X and Reddit. Burnout is the main risk.

### Cited Findings
- Growth Unhinged reached 78K+ subscribers and 4M annual views while Kyle Poyar was working elsewhere, before he went full-time in October 2025. About half of new subscribers came via 500+ recommending publications. — [Growth Unhinged, "A new chapter"](https://kylepoyar.substack.com/p/a-new-chapter); [Thormeier](https://blog.thormeier.co/p/how-to-build-and-monetize-a-newsletter)
- Lenny credits guest posts on First Round Review plus Substack Recommendations for his growth to 500K. — [Growth in Reverse](https://growthinreverse.substack.com/p/the-5-biggest-takeaways-from-lennys)
- Creator burnout: 59% of full-time creators and 42% of hobbyists reported burnout in the past year (Kit, 2024). — [Net Influencer](https://www.netinfluencer.com/convertkit-state-of-the-creator-economy-2024/)
- beehiiv data shows longer-running publications perform better, which supports a compounding, long-horizon commitment. — [beehiiv on X](https://x.com/beehiiv/status/2103209449657885174)

### Inferences (suggested operating model; not sourced data)
- **Anchor (weekly, about 4–6 hrs):** a weekly or biweekly Substack essay with proprietary data or a teardown (for example, TripleDart GTM benchmarks or Slate-powered experiments). Alternatively, a biweekly video podcast with a CMO or founder guest (about 1 hr recording plus editing, outsourced).
- **Atomize (about 2 hrs, AI-assisted with human edit):** 3–5 LinkedIn posts, 3–5 Shorts or clips, a Substack Note thread, and an X thread. Join Reddit only where a genuine answer fits.
- **Network (ongoing):** trade Substack Recommendations and guest posts with adjacent B2B marketing writers. Podcast guests become cross-promoters.
- **IRL (quarterly):** one curated CMO or founder dinner per key city to convert digital audience into relationships and pipeline.
- **Skip or minimal:** Medium (canonical cross-posts only), Bluesky, standalone Shorts without an anchor.

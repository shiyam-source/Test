# Profound vs Slate: Pricing, Plan Tiers, Prompt Quotas, Usage Limits and Contract Terms

Research date: 2026-11-02. **Method caveat (read first):** In this session WebFetch could not reach tryprofound.com, slatehq.com, rankscope.ai or geotoolbox.ai (they are outside the session's allowed network domains), and the Wayback Machine was not tried after those failures. So **no vendor pricing page was read directly**. Everything below comes from web-search result snippets and summaries of third-party reviews and Slate's own blog. Each claim cites the page the snippet came from. Before anything is published, a person should check: (1) tryprofound.com/pricing, both the brand and agency tabs; (2) slatehq.com/pricing; (3) Wayback snapshots of tryprofound.com/pricing for 2025 to 2026. Per-unit costs are my own arithmetic on the reported figures and are labeled as inferences.

## 1. Current and historical Profound plans, list prices, billing and enterprise gating

### Takeaway
Profound's self-serve pricing has changed several times. It launched a $499/mo "Lite" plan in June 2025. Later (2026) it sold $99 "Starter" and $399 "Growth" plans billed annually. Around mid-September 2026 it reportedly removed both brand self-serve plans. As of Oct–Nov 2026, brands get a 7-day free trial and then a custom-quoted Enterprise contract. Agencies still have a self-serve route at $399/mo per client workspace. Wider engine coverage (Claude, Gemini, Copilot and others), SSO, API and tailored prompt volumes are reported as Enterprise-only.

### Inferences
- Over about 16 months Profound moved from a $499 self-serve plan, to a cheaper $99/$399 annual-only ladder, to sales-led only for brands. - The engines buyers increasingly care about (Claude, Gemini, Copilot) sat behind the Enterprise wall even while self-serve tiers existed. Lower-tier buyers were therefore measuring an incomplete picture of AI search.

## 2. Prompt quotas, run frequency, credits and overages, with unit economics

### Takeaway
Profound meters by **unique prompts** (50, 100 or 200 on self-serve and agency tiers) run **daily** on a fixed set of engines. The unit is the prompt, not the answer, and engine count is fixed per tier. On reported figures, Profound's self-serve and agency tiers cost about **$2–$4 per tracked prompt per month**, or about **$0.02–$0.07 per sampled answer**. Enterprise volumes and overage rules are not public.

### Cited Findings
- Starter: 50 prompts, ChatGPT only. Growth: 100 prompts, 3 engines (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews). — [Get-Ryze](https://www.get-ryze.ai/blog/profound-pricing-2026); [That Marketing Buddy](https://thatmarketingbuddy.com/pricing/profound)
- Agency workspace: 100 unique prompts, 3 engines, run daily, $399/mo. — [Tools8020](https://tools8020.com/tools/profound/pricing/); [GeoToolbox](https://geotoolbox.ai/blog/profound-pricing)
- Lite (2025): 24,000 responses analyzed per month = 200 unique prompts. — [Geneo](https://geneo.app/blog/profound-review-2025-with-alternative-recommendation/)
- Trial: 50 prompts daily for 7 days on 3 engines. — [RankScope](https://rankscope.ai/blog/profound-pricing)
- "Enterprise prices, minimums and overages aren't public." — [Trakkr](https://trakkr.ai/reviews/profound-review/pricing)
- Vendr buyer data (reported second-hand): **median Profound contract about $34,500/year, range about $28,000–$61,200**. Not confirmed on Vendr itself. — [Reoptimize](https://reoptimize.ai/profound-pricing)

### Inferences (my arithmetic; assumes daily runs, 30-day month, list price)
| Profound tier (status) | Price/mo | Prompts | Engines | Answers/mo (prompts × engines × 30) | $/prompt/mo | $/answer |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Lite (2025, historical) | $499 | 200 | ~4 implied by 24,000 cap | 24,000 (stated cap) | $2.50 | ~$0.021 |
| Starter (2026, removed Sept 2026) | $99 | 50 | 1 | 1,500 | $1.98 | ~$0.066 |
| Growth (2026, removed Sept 2026) | $399 | 100 | 3 | 9,000 | $3.99 (or $1.33 per prompt-engine) | ~$0.044 |
| Agency workspace (current) | $399 (+$99 base) | 100 | 3 | 9,000 | $3.99 ($4.98 for a single client incl. base) | ~$0.044–$0.055 |
| Enterprise (current, Vendr median) | ~$2,875 ($34,500/yr) | not public | up to ~9–11 | unknown | at $2,875: $28.75/prompt if 100 prompts; $5.75 if 500 | unknown |
- On Growth, going from 1 engine to 3 multiplies answers 3x. Prompt count, the variable that controls statistical coverage of the topic space, stays at 100.
- Under the Vendr median, a brand pays about $2,875/mo before anyone knows its prompt allowance. That is about 7x the old Growth list price.
- Overage behavior (hard stop, throttle or upsell) is undocumented in the sources found.

### Gaps
- No source states Profound's per-engine multiplier or credit system on Enterprise, or what happens when the prompt cap is hit.
- Run frequency on Enterprise (daily versus multiple samples per day) is not confirmed.

## 3. Contract terms reported by buyers

### Takeaway
Self-serve Profound plans were **annual-only**. Brands are now **sales-led only**, with no published Enterprise price, minimums or overage terms. Buyers and reviewers describe the pricing as "confusing and restrictive". Reviewers warn that 12-month commitments to a tracking tool in a fast-changing category are a risk.

### Cited Findings
- Self-serve billing was annual only ($99/$399 "Annual Billing Only"). — [Get-Ryze](https://www.get-ryze.ai/blog/profound-pricing-2026); [Trakkr](https://trakkr.ai/reviews/profound-review/pricing)
- "Profound does not publish an Enterprise price, so budget for a negotiated contract rather than a list price." — [Sprites](https://www.sprites.ai/blog/profound-pricing)
- A Reddit user (quoted second-hand; original thread not found) said Profound "feels average so far, but the pricing is confusing and restrictive", and complained of poor sales responsiveness. — [WorkDuo](https://www.workduo.ai/blog/profound-ai-pricing)
- One review argues that committing to 12 months of a tracking tool means paying for a measurement framework that may need replacing mid-contract. — reported in search summary of [Maintouch "What It Misses"](https://maintouch.com/blogs/profound-ai-review-citations-limits) / [Discovered Labs](https://discoveredlabs.com/blog/profound-ai-visibility-tool-review) (vendor-side source)
- Reviewers advise confirming SOC 2 documentation before signing. — [Maintouch](https://maintouch.com/blogs/profound-ai-pricing)
- Vendr-sourced median of $34,500/yr implies negotiated annual contracts. Vendr has no first-party Profound page that I could find. — [Reoptimize](https://reoptimize.ai/profound-pricing)

### Inferences
- Annual-only billing combined with sales-led Enterprise means a brand cannot buy one month to test Profound beyond the 7-day trial. Seven days of daily runs is too short to see trend or volatility patterns.
- Opaque pricing lets the vendor price by perceived willingness to pay. The reported Enterprise range ($2k–$10k+/mo) is consistent with that.

### Gaps
- No verified data on auto-renewal clauses, notice periods, multi-year discounts or price escalators. No G2 pricing comments were retrieved. Vendr's own Profound data was not accessible.

## 4. How quotas shape buyer behavior (statistical validity, engine trade-offs, agencies)

### Takeaway
AI answers are highly random. A small prompt cap with one sample per day per engine gives noisy visibility numbers. Quota-based pricing pushes buyers to track fewer prompts, drop engines, or restrict client coverage. Agencies pay $399 per client before adding a single extra engine.

### Cited Findings
- SparkToro/Gumshoe study (Jan 2026, Rand Fishkin): there is less than a 1-in-100 chance that ChatGPT or Google's AI returns the same brand list in any two of 100 responses, and less than a 1-in-1,000 chance of the same list in the same order. Volunteers ran 12 prompts about 2,961 times across ChatGPT, Claude and Google AI. Top brands still appeared in 55–77% of responses. — [Search Engine Journal](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/ai-recommendations-change-with-nearly-every-query-sparktoro/566242/); [MediaPost](https://www.mediapost.com/publications/article/412364/ai-brand-recommendations-chaotic-inconsistent.html)
- Human-written prompts for the same intent are very dissimilar to each other (average semantic similarity 0.081). Write-ups recommend running each prompt many times (some cite 60+) and reporting mention frequency rather than rank. — [LLMGeoKit](https://llmgeokit.com/blog/ai-recommendations-inconsistent-what-it-means); [Rampify methodology guide](https://www.rampify.dev/ai-visibility-research-guide)
- Agency executives told Digiday that different AI visibility trackers return different answers to identical prompts. VML was testing about 17 platforms, including Profound, with no clear leader. — [Digiday](https://digiday.com/marketing/marketers-question-expensive-ai-visibility-tools-as-inconsistent-results-fuel-skepticism/)
- Market norm: "fifty to a hundred prompts across three to five engines is the standard small-business parcel", at about $99–189/mo regardless of vendor. — [AI visibility pricing roundup via search](https://airanked.ai/research/ai-visibility-tool-pricing) / [Prefer](https://tryprefer.com/reviews/ai-visibility-tool-pricing/)

### Inferences
- Under Profound Growth or Agency, 100 prompts × 1 run/day gives about 30 samples per prompt per engine per month. That is below the 60+ runs per prompt some methodologists recommend. Week-over-week swings on a prompt can be pure noise, and buyers may act on false signals.
- With prompt-capped pricing, a buyer who wants coverage of the topic space (many prompt phrasings per intent, since human phrasings barely overlap at 0.081 similarity) and statistical depth (many runs per prompt) pays twice. The incentive is to track a small set of "vanity" prompts.
- Agencies: 10 clients on Profound Agency = $99 + 10 × $399 = **$4,089/mo** for 100 prompts × 3 engines each. There is no published way to add Claude or Gemini per client. Agencies are likely to under-cover smaller clients.
- Engine trade-off: on the old ladder, a $99 Starter user saw ChatGPT only. Buyers who wanted Gemini or Claude had to jump to Enterprise.

### Gaps
- No direct buyer testimony (Reddit/G2) about dropping prompts or engines because of quota. This is an inference from the pricing structure, not reported behavior.

## 5. Slate (slatehq.com) pricing and limits

### Takeaway
Slate sells **"unlimited prompts"** and meters **AI answers tracked**, with separate **workflow/execution credits** and **monitored pages**. The current reported entry tier is **Growth at $499/mo** (15,000 credits, 10,000 AI answers/mo, 1,000 tracked pages). Higher tiers reported by third parties are **Scale at $999** and **Agency at $2,000**, plus custom Enterprise. ChatGPT, Google AI Mode, Google AI Overviews and Perplexity are included on all plans. **Claude and Gemini are a paid add-on** (reported $100/mo). Most figures come from Slate's own blog, and tier names have changed over time (Solo/Pro, then Growth/Scale/Agency).

### Cited Findings
- **Current (reported 2026):** Growth $499/mo with 15,000 credits, 10,000 AI answers/month and 1,000 tracked pages. Scale $999/mo. Agency $2,000/mo. Enterprise custom. — [Slate blog: Search Atlas alternatives](https://slatehq.com/blog/search-atlas-alternatives-compared); [Slate blog: Profound pricing](https://slatehq.com/blog/profound-aeo-pricing); third-party [Astiva agencies roundup](https://astiva.ai/blog/best-ai-visibility-tools-for-agencies). The $999 Scale and $2,000 Agency tiers appeared only in third-party snippets and are **unverified on Slate's site**.
- **Historical/older Slate naming:** Solo $199 (unlimited prompts, 4,500 answers tracked, 5,000 workflow credits). Pro $499 (unlimited prompts, 15,000 answers tracked, 15,000 workflow credits). A Slate post notes that stale $199 pricing was being removed, which implies Solo $199 is no longer current. — [Slate blog: Peec AI alternatives](https://slatehq.com/blog/best-peec-ai-alternatives); [Slate vs Profound page](https://slatehq.com/slate-vs-profound)
- **Older podcast/demo description:** Solo $500 for 50,000 credits, Pro $1,000 for 100,000 credits. This was a different credit scale at the time and is a secondhand account. — [Marketing Experts Hub demo with Slate founder](https://b2b.marketingexpertshub.com/p/slate-ai-demo-with-the-founder-of)
- **Enterprise:** 20,000 tracked answers and 50,000–100,000 credits, or "custom limits for prompts, pages and credits". — [Slate vs Profound](https://slatehq.com/slate-vs-profound); [Slate vs Copy.ai](https://slatehq.com/blog/slate-vs-copy-ai)
- **Credits:** consumed each time a workflow runs (content, audits, workflows). They can be topped up without upgrading. The pricing page reportedly lists an add-on of **5,000 credits for $125** ($0.025/credit). Credits, tracked AI answers and monitored pages are **separate allowances**. — search summary of [slatehq.com/pricing](https://slatehq.com/pricing) and [Slate blog](https://slatehq.com/blog/ai-visibility-tools-for-seo-teams)
- **Engines:** ChatGPT, Google AI Mode, Google AI Overviews and Perplexity on every plan. "Add Claude/Gemini for $100/mo." — [Slate vs Profound](https://slatehq.com/slate-vs-profound); [Slate blog: AI search visibility tools](https://slatehq.com/blog/ai-search-visibility-tools)
- **Trial:** 14-day free trial reported, including **30 AI answers** tracked on ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews and Google AI Mode (one Slate source). Whether a card is required was **not confirmed** in any source found. — [Slate blog](https://slatehq.com/blog/profound-aeo-pricing)
- **Agency:** Slate blog confirms "Agency and Enterprise plans are available". Agency price ($2,000/mo) appears only in third-party listings. — [Slate blog: Profound vs AirOps](https://slatehq.com/blog/profound-vs-airops); [Astiva](https://astiva.ai/blog/best-ai-visibility-tools-for-agencies)
- **Name collision:** "Slate by Technolutions" (higher-ed CRM) and slatehq.org (a UGC ad tool, $19–79/mo) are unrelated and should not be confused with slatehq.com. — [SaaS CRM Review](https://saascrmreview.com/slate-by-technolutions-review/); [slatehq.org](https://www.slatehq.org/)

### Inferences (my arithmetic)
- **Slate Growth $499:** 10,000 answers means about **$0.050/answer** if all $499 is attributed to tracking. The plan also bundles 15,000 credits, worth $375 at the $0.025 top-up rate. Net of the credit value, tracking costs about $0.012/answer. Prompts are unlimited, so cost per prompt depends on how the buyer allocates answers. For example, 100 prompts × 4 engines × 25 runs = 10,000 answers, which is **$4.99/prompt/mo** with 25 samples per prompt-engine per month. Alternatively, 333 prompts × 1 engine × 30 runs = **$1.50/prompt/mo**.
- **Like-for-like with Profound Growth/Agency ($399, 9,000 answers on 3 engines, ~$0.044/answer):** Slate's per-answer price is similar ($0.050), but Slate adds a 4th engine (Google AI Mode), unlimited prompts, 1,000 monitored pages and execution credits. Profound's $399 covered tracking only (plus about 6 articles on Growth). The displacement angle is not "Slate is cheaper per answer". It is: "same tracking spend, but you choose how to split answers between prompt breadth and sampling depth, and execution is included."
- **Agency comparison:** 10 clients on Profound Agency = $4,089/mo. Slate's reported Agency plan is $2,000/mo; its allowances were not found, so a per-client comparison cannot be made yet.
- **Claude/Gemini:** Slate gates these as a $100/mo add-on. Profound gates them to Enterprise (~$2k+/mo). Both charge extra, but Slate's add-on is published and self-serve.
- **Brief's internal range "$499–$2,999/mo":** public snippets support $499 to $2,000 plus custom Enterprise. **$2,999 was not found in any source.** Confirm against the live pricing page.

### Gaps
- slatehq.com/pricing could not be fetched. Tier names, Scale/Agency allowances, trial length (14 vs 15 days), the no-card policy and the Claude/Gemini add-on price need confirmation on the live page.
- No independent buyer reviews of Slate pricing or contract terms (monthly vs annual, auto-renew) were found.

## 6. Market norms: how 3–5 other competitors price prompts

### Takeaway
Most competitors publish self-serve prices and meter by prompts (Otterly, Peec, Scrunch) or by responses/credits (AthenaHQ, Ahrefs custom prompts). Entry tiers start at $29–$300/mo. Extra engines (Claude, Gemini, AI Mode) are commonly paid add-ons. Against this norm, Profound's removal of published brand pricing stands out.

### Cited Findings (all as reported in 2026 roundups; verify on each vendor page)
- **Otterly.AI:** Lite $29/mo for 15 prompts. Standard $189 for 100 prompts. Premium $489 for 400 prompts. Claude, AI Mode and Gemini are paid add-ons. — [Prefer pricing roundup](https://tryprefer.com/reviews/ai-visibility-tool-pricing/); [Otterly vs Profound](https://otterly.ai/alternatives/pricing/otterly-vs-profound-pricing/)
- **Peec AI:** Starter $95/mo for 50 prompts on 3 models. Pro $245 for 150. Advanced $495 for 350. A conflicting source lists €89 for 25 prompts and €199 for 100. — [AI Ranked](https://airanked.ai/research/ai-visibility-tool-pricing); [RecoProof](https://recoproof.com/guides/ai-visibility-tool-pricing)
- **Scrunch AI:** Starter $300/mo ($250 billed annually), 350 prompts, 3 seats. A conflicting source lists Core at $250 with 125 prompts and 5 users. — [AI Visibility Compare](https://aivisibilitycompare.com/guides/ai-visibility-tool-pricing.html); [Cheers](https://www.cheers.tech/geo-academy/how-much-does-ai-visibility-software-cost)
- **AthenaHQ:** credit-based. Starter $295/mo ($245 annual) for 3,600 credits, with 1 credit = 1 AI response, so **~$0.082/response**. Free plan with 300 credits. — [Arahi](https://arahi.ai/blog/best-ai-visibility-tools-2026); [Loamly](https://www.loamly.ai/blog/comparison-ai-search-visibility-tools-2026-buyers-guide)
- **Semrush AI Visibility Toolkit:** $99/mo per domain. Semrush One bundle reported at $199/mo. — [Exploding Topics](https://explodingtopics.com/blog/ai-visibility-vs-brand-radar); [Rework](https://resources.rework.com/tools/aeo-ai-visibility/semrush-ai-toolkit-vs-ahrefs-brand-radar)

Custom prompts start at $50/mo for 2,500 checks (**$0.02/check**). — [Sightivo](https://sightivo.com/blog/ahrefs-brand-radar-vs-semrush-ai-visibility-toolkit); [Omid Saffari cost math](https://omidsaffari.com/blog/growth-brand-radar-vs-semrush-ai-visibility-cost-math)

### Inferences (my arithmetic)
- Per tracked prompt per month at list price: Otterly Standard about $1.89, Peec Starter about $1.90, Peec Advanced about $1.41, Scrunch Starter about $0.71–$0.86, old Profound Growth about $3.99, Profound Agency workspace about $3.99. At the old self-serve level, Profound was at the top of the market per prompt. It now publishes no brand price at all.
- Per answer: Ahrefs custom checks about $0.02, Profound Lite (2025) about $0.021, Profound Growth/Agency about $0.044, Slate Growth about $0.050 gross (about $0.012 net of bundled credits), AthenaHQ about $0.082.

### Gaps
- Competitor figures come from roundups with different snapshot dates. Run frequency (daily vs weekly) differs by vendor and was not normalized, so the per-answer comparisons are approximate.

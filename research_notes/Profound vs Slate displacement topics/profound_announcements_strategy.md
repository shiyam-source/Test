## 1. Funding rounds, investors, valuations, and what founders and investors said

### Takeaway
Profound raised about $335M across roughly five rounds in about 27 months. Its valuation went from unicorn ($1B, Feb 2026) to $1.8B seven months later (Sep 2026), with Kleiner Perkins, Sequoia and Lightspeed taking turns leading. Each round's press language shifted the company's self-description: "Answer Engine Optimization" (Series A) became "connecting brands with Superintelligence" (Series B), then "a new category of marketing" (Series C), then "the AI platform for marketing teams" (Series D). That is a steady move away from being a visibility tracker.

### Inferences
- Customer-count claims rose from 700+ enterprises (Feb 2026) to 1,000+ "enterprise brands" (Sep 2026), about 43% in seven months, while the valuation rose 80%. Profound has never disclosed ARR, so the valuation step-up rests on logo-count and Fortune-list metrics rather than revenue. That makes these claims fair targets for scrutiny in displacement content: "1/3 of the Fortune 100" says nothing about seat depth, contract size or renewal.
- The "Fortune 500" metric moved from "more than 10%" (Feb) to "16%" (Sep). The headline switched to the Fortune 100 (one third ≈ 33 companies), likely because it reads better.
- Investors kept rotating the lead (KP, then Sequoia, then Lightspeed, then Sequoia+KP), and every insider kept participating. That points to high insider conviction and also to a heavily capitalized competitor. Profound can subsidize enterprise pilots and agency programs (see section 3).
- The narrative arc runs from "AEO" (2025) to "Superintelligence as a customer" (Aug 2025), "brands need their own Agents" (Feb 2026) and "AI platform for marketing teams" (Sep 2026). The monitoring category Profound helped create is no longer its pitch. It now competes with horizontal AI-marketing suites and with execution platforms like Slate.

- No seed-round details (date, investors, amount).
- No verified ARR, net revenue retention, or a headcount after the Series D.
- Dylan Babbs (co-founder) had no public statements in the results.

## 2. Product launches timeline (with dates and claims)

### Takeaway
The product went in roughly 18 months from a visibility tracker (Answer Engine Insights) to data products (Conversation Explorer/Prompt Volumes, Agent Analytics, Shopping, MCP), to Agents (build-your-own, templates, marketplace), to an always-on orchestrator (Aim, Jul 2026), to an "AI Marketer" with Projects and SEO data (Oct 2026). Every major 2026 release is about execution, not measurement.

### Cited Findings
**2024 to 2025: monitoring and data layer**
- Answer Engine Insights (core visibility tracking): the original product. No dated launch announcement was found. By the Series A it was tracking "more than 100 million AI search queries every month" — [BuiltIn NYC](https://www.builtinnyc.com/articles/profound-raises-20m-series-a-20250625)
- Agent Analytics: launched March 2025, per Profound's blog feed. It tracks how AI crawlers and answer engines interact with a site. A later post covers a one-click Vercel Marketplace integration — [Profound blog via search summary](https://www.tryprofound.com/blog/technology); [Profound blog index](https://www.tryprofound.com/blog)
- Profound Lite (self-serve for startups and SMBs): announced alongside the June 2025 Series A, per Sacra and other summaries. A 2026 pricing search found no current "Lite" plan, so it was probably renamed into the Starter/Growth tiers — [Sacra](https://sacra.com/c/profound/); [geotally](https://geotally.ai/blog/profound-pricing)
- Conversation Explorer / Prompt Volumes: estimated AI "search volume" from double-opt-in consumer panels. Conversation Explorer lets users search the full conversations behind the volume numbers. No exact launch date was found; it is described as established by 2025 — [Nick Lafferty review](https://nicklafferty.com/reviews/profound-best-aeo-geo-platform-for-ai-search/); [Nick Lafferty, Prompt Volume explainer](https://nicklafferty.com/blog/prompt-volume-aeo-explained/)
- ChatGPT Shopping tracking: shows product visibility in ChatGPT Shopping and which competing products and retailers appear. Exact date not found (2025) — [Profound blog index](https://www.tryprofound.com/blog); [generatemore.ai review](https://generatemore.ai/blog/my-profound-ai-search-visibility-review-for-saas-/-tech)
- MCP server: launched October 2025. It connects ChatGPT, Claude Desktop, Cursor and Cline to Profound's visibility, sentiment, citation, ChatGPT Shopping and Agent Analytics data. Hosted at mcp.tryprofound.com/mcp with OAuth, Enterprise-only. Later updates added write actions for prompts, agents, knowledge bases, documents and projects — [Profound blog "The Profound MCP Evolution"](https://www.tryprofound.com/blog/the-profound-mcp-evolution-part-1); [Profound blog "Bring Profound data directly into your AI workflow with MCP"](https://www.tryprofound.com/blog/bring-profound-data-directly-into-your-ai-workflow-with-mcp); [Profound docs](https://docs.tryprofound.com/mcp/connection-tutorials/connect-chatgpt); [LLM Pulse comparison](https://llmpulse.ai/blog/best-mcp-servers-ai-visibility/)
- Agents (early form): one third-party review says Agents first shipped in late 2025 — [Analyze AI review](https://www.tryanalyze.ai/blog/profound-ai-review)

**2026: execution layer**
- Feb 24 2026, Profound Agents (formal launch with the Series C): build your own agents or deploy Agent Templates. Starter and Growth plans got 100 free agent runs for a month. "Over 500 customers now use Profound Agents daily." Profound said the raise funds "two major initiatives launching today" — [Profound changelog](https://product.tryprofound.com/changelog/announcing-our-series-c); [Profound newsroom](https://www.tryprofound.com/newsroom/profound-raises-series-c-at-1b-valuation-to-lead-a-new-category-of-marketing)

Release headline: "Profound Launches Aim to Transform AI Search Data into Marketing Execution." - Oct 2 2026, AEO Content Score (ML score for how likely content is to be cited by AI search) and Dynamic Bot Rendering (serves pre-rendered pages to AI bots) — [Profound changelog](https://product.tryprofound.com/changelog)

- Oct 19 2026, Search Visibility in Pages: adds Moz ranking data next to AI visibility, so the AI Marketer can flag gaps between rankings and citations — [Profound changelog](https://product.tryprofound.com/changelog)
- Also in Oct 2026: Profound Teams setup, and a Bazaarvoice integration — [Profound changelog](https://product.tryprofound.com/changelog)
- One reviewer describes the arc as going "From AI Visibility Tracker to AI Marketer" — [generatemore.ai](https://generatemore.ai/blog/my-profound-ai-search-visibility-review-for-saas-/-tech)

### Inferences
- "AI Marketer" (Oct 2026) is now the product's central persona and the primary interface. Agents, Projects and Pages are framed as things "your AI Marketer" does. That puts Profound in direct conflict with Slate's execution positioning; it is no longer an adjacent analytics tool.
- Bringing in Moz rankings (Oct 19 2026) is a quiet concession that classic SEO data still matters. Profound is rebuilding an SEO-plus-AEO suite through a partner's data instead of owning it, which leaves a data dependency on Moz.
- The Agents product appears to have been rebuilt at least twice in about 8 months: templates (Feb), Assistant and Background Agents (roadmap), Aim (Jul), then conversational AI Marketer agents (Oct). Fast iteration also means churn in UX and concepts for enterprise users, so expect change-management fatigue and training debt.
- Dynamic Bot Rendering and next-aeo move Profound into technical delivery (rendering and site infrastructure). That overlaps with Vercel, Cloudflare and CDN players, and with Slate's site-level execution.

### Gaps
- No primary-source launch dates for Answer Engine Insights, Conversation Explorer, Prompt Volumes or Shopping (the blog was unreachable).
- No "Profound Index" product or report was found under that name.
- Aim pricing and limits come only from a third-party blog (Digital Applied) and are unverified.
- Unknown whether Dynamic Bot Rendering is GA or beta, or how it is priced.

## 3. Strategic shifts: execution, down-market vs. enterprise, partnerships, hires, logos

### Takeaway
Profound is pushing upmarket (Fortune 100, GSIs like Accenture and Deloitte, Enterprise-only MCP and engines) and into execution (Agents, Aim, AI Marketer) at the same time. Its self-serve brand tier looks like it is being cut back in favor of agencies. The agency and GSI channel is now a formal, staffed motion.

### Cited Findings
- Enterprise gating: MCP access is Enterprise-only. Claude tracking and wider engine coverage are reported as Enterprise-only. Starter covers ChatGPT only (50 prompts, 1 seat); Growth covers ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews (100 prompts, 3 seats) — [geotally](https://geotally.ai/blog/profound-pricing); [get-ryze](https://www.get-ryze.ai/blog/profound-pricing-2026); [LLM Pulse](https://llmpulse.ai/blog/best-mcp-servers-ai-visibility/)
- Starter $99 and Growth $399 were billed annually only ("the pricing page has no monthly toggle at all") — [get-ryze](https://www.get-ryze.ai/blog/profound-pricing-2026)
- At one point the public brand pricing page listed only Trial and Enterprise; the $99 and $399 plans appeared only in in-app checkout after sign-up — [citeprism](https://citeprism.com/blog/profound-pricing)
- Reported mid-Sep 2026 change (single source): the brand $99 Starter plan was removed from the pricing page. The page now shows a $99/month "Agency Growth" plan with $399/month per additional client workspace — [maintouch, Oct 2026](https://maintouch.com/blogs/profound-ai-pricing) (unverified against Profound's site)

"Build and scale a Global Systems Integrator (GSI) program, establishing and deepening relationships with the likes of Accenture, Deloitte, Bain, Cap Gemini etc." — [Ashby job post](https://jobs.ashbyhq.com/Profound/816c2ab6-7398-4cd8-b1b5-695d4673b476)

- Agent adoption claims: "about 60% of the orgs on Profound are using agents regularly now" (Cadwallader, Knuckle Up podcast); "over 500 customers now use Profound Agents daily" (Feb 2026) — [Knuckle Up](https://www.knuckleup.co/episodes/james-cadwallader); [Pulse2](https://pulse2.com/profound-96-million-series-c-raised-at-1-billion-valuation-for-ai-native-marketing-platform/)

### Inferences
- The partner motion has grown from boutique AEO agencies (iPullRank, Avenue Z, Greenpark) to a planned GSI program (Accenture, Deloitte, Bain, Capgemini). Profound is building a channel for Fortune 500 transformation budgets. Those deals are slow, services-heavy and implemented by the GSI, which opens room for a faster, more self-serve execution platform at mid-market and growth-stage companies.
- If the brand Starter tier really was removed in Sep 2026 and replaced with an agency plan, then mid-market and SMB brands are being sent to agencies rather than served directly. That gap is open to any competitor with a usable self-serve execution plan.
- The tension: Agents and Aim are pitched as letting a marketing team "take on" more work itself, while the channel strategy pays agencies and GSIs to do that work for clients. Over time, Profound's own AI Marketer and its partners' services will cannibalize each other.
- Enterprise-only gating of Claude tracking and MCP clashes with the "superintelligence is your customer" vision. Non-Enterprise customers can't see one of the major models, which undercuts the claim that brands need complete coverage.

### Gaps
- No evidence was found of layoffs, executive departures, a CRO or CMO hire, or other named leadership hires.
- No confirmed partnerships with Cloudflare or OpenAI, beyond the "OpenAI Ads" integration node.
- Ramp and Figma could not be confirmed as Profound customers. A Ramp co-founder (Karim Atiyeh) is an angel investor, which is a different thing.
- No data-licensing deal was found, although the panel-based prompt data could support one.

## 4. Public research and data claims, and critiques

### Takeaway
Profound's best-known research is its citation-pattern study (680M citations), which is widely cited by the industry, and it markets panel-derived "prompt volumes" as a moat. Independent reviewers, most of them rivals, question whether anyone can measure prompt volume accurately. Profound's own dataset-size claims vary widely across its pages.

### Cited Findings
- "AI Platform Citation Patterns" (Aug 2025 update): about 680M citations across ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews and Perplexity, Aug 2024 to Jun 2025. Wikipedia is ChatGPT's top source (7.8% of all citations; 47.9% of the top-10 share). Reddit leads Perplexity (6.6%) and Google AIO (2.2%) and is second on ChatGPT (1.8%) — [Profound blog](https://www.tryprofound.com/blog/ai-platform-citation-patterns); [BuzzStream roundup](https://www.buzzstream.com/blog/top-sites-chatgpt/)
- Secondary claim attributed to Profound tracking: LinkedIn climbed from about #11 on ChatGPT (Nov 2025) to #5 (Feb 2026) — [Contently, Apr 29 2026](https://contently.com/2026/04/29/top-sources-llms-cite/)
- Methodology claim: prompt data comes from double-opt-in consumer panels, captured from the browser-rendered consumer experience, with "no synthetic prompts" — [Trakkr data-accuracy review](https://trakkr.ai/reviews/profound-review/data-accuracy); [Nick Lafferty review](https://nicklafferty.com/reviews/profound-best-aeo-geo-platform-for-ai-search/)
- Dataset-size claims conflict: "over 130 million real user conversations", "1.5B+ real user conversations/prompts" and "400M+ real user conversations" all appear in different sources — [Trakkr](https://trakkr.ai/reviews/profound-review/data-accuracy); [Nick Lafferty](https://nicklafferty.com/blog/prompt-volume-aeo-explained/)
- Critique (from a rival AEO tool's co-founder): having the best "prompt volume data" doesn't mean it is much more accurate, because "nobody is currently reporting prompt volumes accurately", and "literally nobody can provide real data to support these estimates… including Profound" — [Cairrot](https://cairrot.com/alternatives/profound-review-price-comparison-top-alternatives/)
- Critique: "prompt search volume has no reliable database" — [Jaeckert-O'Daniel](https://www.jaeckert-odaniel.com/en/prompt-search-volume-real-data-or-all-guessed/)
- Critique: Profound's public statements on data freshness and latency conflict — [Trakkr](https://trakkr.ai/reviews/profound-review/data-accuracy)
- Prompt Volumes are described as "probabilistic modeling on tens of millions of real conversations" — [Nick Lafferty](https://nicklafferty.com/blog/prompt-volume-aeo-explained/)

### Inferences
- Over roughly 18 months, Profound's headline data scale went from 100M queries a month (2025) to "1.5B+ conversations". Those numbers measure different things (queries tracked vs. panel conversations), and Profound doesn't reconcile them. Content can ask exactly what is being counted.
- Prompt Volumes are modeled extrapolations from a panel, not logs from the AI providers. Any "search volume for AI" claim carries that uncertainty, so a competitor can argue for prioritizing action on observed citations over modeled demand.
- The citation study's data ends in June 2025. Its Wikipedia-heavy findings may be stale given later engine changes, including Profound's own LinkedIn-rise observation.

### Gaps
- No "AI search share" market report or "Profound Index" publication from Profound was found.
- No independent academic or journalistic audit of Profound's panel was found. Most critiques come from competitors with a commercial interest.

## 5. Controversies, criticism, founder hot takes

### Takeaway

## 6. Where the stated vision contradicts product or pricing reality

### Takeaway
The vision is broad ("the AI platform for marketing teams", "connect brands with Superintelligence"). The reality is enterprise gating, annual-only billing, ChatGPT-only entry tiers, Claude locked to Enterprise, a self-serve tier that may have been removed, modeled (not observed) demand data, and an agent product redesigned about every quarter.

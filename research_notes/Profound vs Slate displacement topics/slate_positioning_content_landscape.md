# Slate vs Profound: Public Positioning and the "Profound Alternatives / vs" Content Landscape

Research date: 2026-11-02. Method note: direct page fetches of slatehq.com and tryprofound.com were blocked in this research environment (DNS not on the session allow-list), so every finding below comes from web-search result snippets and summaries of the cited URLs, not full-page reads. Treat page-level claims as "as indexed by search," and re-verify exact wording on the live pages before publishing. I could not query ChatGPT or Perplexity directly; the "ranking pages" lists reflect web search results only. "INTERNAL" marks claims from the Slate team brief that I could not verify publicly.

## Q1. What does slatehq.com say today, and does Slate already have Profound comparison or alternatives content?

### Takeaway
Yes, and a lot of it. Slate already owns a dedicated `/slate-vs-profound` page plus at least six Profound-centred blog posts (alternatives, pricing, and vs Scrunch, AirOps, Peec and Ahrefs). Its main argument is "Profound observes, Slate executes." That angle is now weaker because Profound has launched AI Marketer, Agents and Ads Studio (see Q4). New Slate content has to go past "execution vs monitoring" or it will repeat both Slate's own pages and the wider landscape.

### Inferences (candidate untaken angles, ranked by defensibility)
1. **Correcting how others describe you off-site.** No page found covers fixing what competitor "alternatives" listicles and directories (G2, Capterra, Crunchbase) say about your brand.

## How plans were chosen

- I used the entry plan, or the closest match to Slate Growth for one brand. I didn't pick premium tiers to inflate the total.
- **AI visibility:** Slate Growth gives 10,000 AI answers a month. Peec Pro (150 prompts × 3 models × daily ≈ 13,500 answers) is the closest match, so I used it instead of Peec Advanced ($495).
- **Ahrefs Lite** tracks 750 keywords, the same number as Slate Growth.
- **Clearscope Essentials** covers 50 pages, against Slate's 1,000 monitored pages. The comparison is conservative.

## Check before posting

1. **Ahrefs.** Slate also connects to Ahrefs and Semrush. The FAQ says "Ahrefs and Semrush included at no extra cost", but it doesn't say whether customers still need their own Ahrefs account for keyword data. If they do, remove the Ahrefs row (the total becomes $1,501).
2. **Not on the image on purpose:** paid media / Google Ads and landing page building. Slate's FAQ says it is a poor fit for teams that only grow through paid ads, and /pages is a performance view, not a page builder.
3. **Looser matches:** Databox is a full BI tool and Crayon is a sales-enablement CI platform. Slate covers the reporting and competitor-research jobs, not everything those tools do. The post says this openly.
4. **Enterprise estimates.** Profound ($20K+/yr), AirOps ($30K+/yr) and Crayon ($30K/yr) aren't published prices. The image and post label them "est." and call them low-end. If you have real quotes from prospects or customers, use those instead.
5. **Prices change.** Re-check on posting day if it is more than a week after 11 Nov 2026.
6. **Logos** are the vendors' own favicons and app icons, downloaded from their sites. Using them to identify the products in a factual price comparison is standard practice, but your call.
